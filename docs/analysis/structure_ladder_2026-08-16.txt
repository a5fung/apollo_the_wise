READING THE WHOLE PICTURE TOGETHER (added after all three passes — not a new test, a synthesis)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Zero of 15 pre-registered tests survive Bonferroni adjustment — nothing here clears the formal
bar. The post-hoc dose table [C] and Spearman rhos [F] point the SAME direction, consistently:
zones_cleared trends NEGATIVELY with excursion size (falls roughly monotonically 0 -> 1-2 -> 3-5
-> 6+ for both horizons; both horizons' rhos are negative, -0.11/-0.14, vs gap_pct's near-zero,
inconsistent-sign rhos, +0.07/+0.02/-0.12) — the OPPOSITE of "the more it clears the stronger."
The ADR check in [G] shows this is PARTLY, not fully, a volatility artifact: the decline survives
inside the low-ADR half of the population but goes flat/noisy inside the high-ADR half (small
cells, n=23-27). None of this clears formal multiplicity adjustment, and the strongest single
number (D20/H, raw p=0.015-0.034) is one look among 19 total, unadjusted. The defensible summary:
raw gap % looks close to noise here (consistent with his hypothesis about gap % specifically),
but zones_cleared is not the confirmed stronger replacement — the data leans toward a MILD
REVERSAL, strongest among lower-volatility names, not proven, and not something to act on without
a larger or independent sample.

CAVEATS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Population = mi_ep_missed_outcomes, source IN (moderate_alert, high_unentered): EPs that
  reached alert grade (HIGH/MODERATE, mi_ep_alerts, source='live') and were never entered. This
  is the DECLINED population — every alert that was actually traded is absent by the table's own
  design (documented already in _skip_attribution_read.py / _rs_inflection_read.py). This data
  cannot say how zones_cleared behaves on our own trades.
- MFE in R units (under the live ORB geometry) was explicitly SKIPPED — see the script's
  docstring for why _468_moderate_realized_r.py could not supply it without re-deriving its ORB
  fill/trigger logic (a "fifth reconstruction," which the task ruled out). max_high_5d/20d (%
  from the alert-day open, not R) carried the excursion-size question instead.
- Tier-A (minute bars present, needed for zones_cleared_and_held) covers 235 of 526 rows (45%);
  the rest predate intraday capture or are thin names; test 10 runs on that smaller subset.
- gap_pct and the tercile/median splits are computed from the analyzed population's OWN
  distribution (outcome-blind); zones_cleared's 0-vs->=1 split was fixed before any outcome was
  read (declared in the script docstring at first commit).
- BLUE_SKY / "nothing overhead" is relative to each name's AVAILABLE daily history in this pull
  (median ~13 months, range 17-279 trading days), not a literal all-time high.
- No valid session-clustered permutation p was computed for the continuous Spearman
  correlations in [F] — they are reported as descriptive point estimates only, to avoid
  presenting an ad hoc significance claim under time pressure. The formal, adjusted-significance
  reads are the 15-test pre-registered battery above; [F] supports the direction/consistency
