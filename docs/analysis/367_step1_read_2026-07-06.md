## The fork — RESOLVED: **self-referential corpora, NOT a matcher bug**

The 7/4-eve diagnostic left two hypotheses for the persistent 0% name-attribution: (1) matcher
bug, or (2) the corpora genuinely don't contain peer company names. Settled empirically 7/6 by
inspecting concrete corpora:

- **AVAV** (Defense Drones & UAS): grounded_text = "AeroVironment Inc … 0001368622 … FORM 8-K" —
  AVAV's OWN 8-K. Names AeroVironment (itself) + SEC boilerplate; no peer (KTOS/RCAT/ONDS) names.
- **MU** (AI Memory & Storage): "mu-20260624 … 0000723125 … FORM 8-K" — Micron's own filing; no
  WDC/STX/SNDK.
- **WULF** (Crypto Asset Recovery): WULF's own 8-K + boilerplate.

`compute_name_attribution` (theme_axis_shadow.py) is structurally correct (normalize names →
word-bounded search). Its own docstring even states the assumption — "grounded_text is the raw
8-K (names peers by company name)". That assumption is FALSE: a company's 8-K about its own
events does not name its competitors. **Signal (a) is aimed at the wrong text source.** The
matcher needs no fix; the CORPUS needs to be a theme-narrative source, not the subject's filing.
