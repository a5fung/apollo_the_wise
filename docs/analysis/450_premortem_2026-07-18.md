## F1 — Correlated book gaps down overnight (TOP RISK, unchanged; mitigations now partial)

**Mechanism.** 5 slots × 20%-of-equity cap (`MAX_CONCURRENT_LIVE_POSITIONS=5`,
`constants.py:260`; 20% cap `order_manager.py:125-128`) = up to ~100% notional deployed. The
theme axis *steers into* same-family entries (ADR 0015 credits Accelerating-theme membership
toward HIGH), and multi-day holds (SMA-trail exit, `exit_logic.apply_daily_exit_step`) carry
that book overnight with GTC stop-market orders (`alpaca_client.py:352`). Stops are floors, not
prevention: a sector-wide −15–25% gap opens BELOW every stop; stop-market fills at the open
print. One open = −15–25% of account, jumping straight past the breaker's −12% BLOCK tier
(which evaluates at 16:12 ET the *next* day, `scheduler.py:1400`) and past the 2% daily-loss
gate (see F2 — it wouldn't even count these losses).

**Current mitigation.**
- `exposure_family.shadow_check_and_emit` (`exposure_family.py:69-102`, hook at
  `entry_pipeline.py:383-391`) — SHADOW-only, emits audit+Telegram when an entry would be the
  3rd same-family position. **Never blocks.**
- `book_concentration.py` 16:18 ET digest (#452 stage-1, verify-live 7/17) — EOD telemetry.
- `premarket_gap_risk_scan` 9:00 ET (`scheduler.py:1174`) — read-only heads-up, fires AFTER the
  gap exists.
- Per-position stops (GTC) + `gap_through_telemetry.py` (measurement only).

**Residual gap.** Everything above is visibility; nothing has authority. The family key v1 =
active-theme membership — tickers correlated by sector/beta but not co-themed (e.g. five
small-cap momentum names in a risk-off tape) share no "family" and are invisible even to the
shadow counter. No aggregate-overnight-notional cap exists at all: the book can be 100%
deployed into a single macro bet with every individual gate green.

**Hardening (design only).**
1. ⚖ Promote the family-slot cap to blocking (`block:theme_concentration` at
   `_check_safeguards`) — already designed to execution depth in
   `docs/analysis/452_r1_stage2_design_2026-07-17.md`; the `exposure_family_cap_promotion`
   review date-fires **7/27**. This is the single highest-leverage flip in the whole register.
2. ⚖ Consider a second, family-blind axis for the same gate: max aggregate overnight notional
   as %-of-equity (e.g. new entry blocked when open notional > X%), because the family key
   cannot see beta-correlation. Surface as an operator fork at the same 7/27 sitting — one
   decision, two axes.
3. No-money now: extend `book_concentration` digest with the E-bucket (ADR 0032 ecosystems)
   rollup so "3 positions, 3 themes, 1 ecosystem" is visible — the stems-vs-ecosystems fork
   (F-D) is already in the stage-2 design.

**PLAN refs:** #452 (stage-2 gate = the open item), #479 (digest surface).

## F2 — The daily-loss brake doesn't count the losses that matter (fail-open by keying)

**Mechanism.** The 2% daily-loss gate sums `total_pnl` over
`WHERE alert_date = $1 AND status='closed' AND total_pnl < 0` (`live_tracker.py:176-181`).
`alert_date` is the ENTRY date. So a loss realized *today* on a position entered any prior day
— i.e. **exactly the overnight-gap / trailed-winner-giveback class, the largest same-day loss
source in a multi-day-hold methodology** — is invisible to the gate. Concrete: four Day-2+
positions gap down at the open and stop out for −6% realized; at 9:31 a fresh HIGH fires;
`_check_safeguards` computes today's losses = $0 and admits it at full size. The SSoT says
"sum of **today's closed-trade** total_pnl" (`docs/setups/safeguards.md` §5) — the code
implements "today's-**alerted** closed trades." SSoT and code have diverged; no change-log
entry marks the keying as deliberate. Two lesser siblings: (a) the gate is evaluated only at
the next entry attempt (no standing intraday actor — partially covered by #455 stage-1
alerts); (b) it counts only *realized* losses, so an open book down −5% MTM blocks nothing
(by design — but note it composes with (a)).

**Current mitigation.** For same-day entries that stop out same-day (the Day-1 dominant flow)
the gate works as intended. The intraday drawdown alert (`intraday_drawdown.py`, 15-min
piggyback, live-only, alert-only) now surfaces MTM bleed ≥ WATCH/REDUCE tiers. The EOD breaker
catches the equity damage the *next* day at 16:12.

**Residual gap.** On the worst plausible morning (gap-down on a held book), the one safeguard
designed for "catastrophic intraday backstop" is structurally blind, and the breaker won't see
it until 16:12. The window where R1's damage lands is precisely the window with no counting
brake.

**Hardening (design only).** ⚖ Re-key the daily-loss sum on close date:
`(closed_at AT TIME ZONE 'America/New_York')::date = today` (the column already exists — the
circuit-breaker query reads `closed_at`, `live_tracker.py:198-203`). This is a safeguard
semantics change → CHANGE_PROCESS + safeguards.md change-log + operator sign-off, but it is a
*restoration of the documented intent*, not a new threshold. Verify with a replay of historical
close days (how often would the corrected gate have bound?). **No PLAN # exists for this — new
finding; needs filing.**

## F4 — The breaker's fail-open stack: the brakes disengage exactly under infra stress

**Mechanism.** Three independent fail-opens can silently disable the tiered breaker:
1. **Stale snapshots**: if the most recent equity snapshot is >48h old,
   `sufficient_history=False` and active-phase reads keep the last persisted state
   (`drawdown_breaker.py:286-293`; `_STALE_DATA_HOURS=48` :78) — a dead 16:12 cron for 3 days
   = breaker frozen at its old tier while equity slides.
2. **DB read error**: `read_breaker_state` returns `'OK'` on ANY exception
   (`drawdown_breaker.py:475-494`) → multiplier 1.0, full size.
3. **Alpaca fetch failure at recompute**: state simply doesn't transition
   (`recompute_drawdown_state` → `drawdown_check_unavailable`, :309-328).
Infra stress (API brownouts, DB contention) correlates with MARKET stress — the fail-open
direction is anti-correlated with when you need the brake. Same family: `vix=None` in the
regime record → sizing silently reverts to FULL base risk (`constants.py:35-36` — the
docstring calls this "conservative fallback"; it is the opposite in a vol spike where the VIX
ingest is the thing that broke). Also documented-unmitigated: a live-account withdrawal reads
as drawdown, a deposit raises the peak (safeguards.md:87).

**Current mitigation (real, post-7/12 red-team).** `drawdown_check_unavailable` is explicitly
bucketed + Telegrammed by the nightly silent-error surfacer with a "breaker FAIL-OPEN" line
(`scheduler.py:751-802`); the 16:12 job is watched; entry-time account-fetch failure fails
CLOSED (`live_tracker.py:160-165`, blocks the entry); `/pause` halt-state unreadable fails
CLOSED (`INFRA_HALT_STATE_UNREADABLE`, :111-115). The daily-loss and cap checks don't depend on
the snapshot table at all.

**Residual gap.** Case 2 (DB read error → OK) has no alert at the read site — it logs a
warning and sizes full; and cases 1–3 are *detected* but nothing escalates a persistent
fail-open into a mechanical consequence (the breaker can stay effectively disabled for days on
an acknowledged-but-unfixed alert).

**Hardening (design only).** ⚖ Operator fork: should a *persistent* fail-open (e.g. ≥2
consecutive `drawdown_check_unavailable` days, or `read_breaker_state` erroring at entry-time)
fail CLOSED for the live mode (block new entries, like the halt-state precedent) instead of
open? The halt-state and account-fetch guards already established the fail-closed pattern for
live; the breaker is the outlier. Cheap alternative if that's too aggressive: an entry-time
`read_breaker_state` exception at least emits an audit row (today it's container-log-only).
**PLAN ref:** none exact — nearest is #456(b) F16 sweep (same empty-vs-error class); needs its
own line if adopted.

## F5 — Stop integrity residuals: the silent-stop class is well-covered, the LOUD-but-naked class is not

**Mechanism & current mitigation.** The MRAM/GOOGL silent-stop class is now 4-layer covered:
stop-ACK 30s watchdog (`scheduler.py:1552`, predicate `stop_order_id IS NULL`), 15-min
order-status reconcile (`order_manager.py:2566-2683`), the never-naked coverage invariant
`_ensure_stop_coverage` (:2813+, broker-truth sizing, adopt-don't-duplicate :2731) and
morning_stop_refresh 9:35. Day-1 mechanics: the OTO bracket is DAY-TIF
(`alpaca_client.py:301`) with a verified stop leg — a bracket that comes back legless is
cancelled and raised (`alpaca_client.py:310-325`), never filled naked.

**Residual gaps (three, each concrete).**
1. **Gap-through converges to naked-with-one-alert *by design*.** When price is already through
   the stop, Alpaca rejects the re-place ("stop must be less than current price") and the
   invariant deliberately CONVERGES — one alert, no retry, no market-out, position stays naked
   pending the operator (`_STOP_ABOVE_MARKET_SIGNATURES`, `order_manager.py:2797-2810`; real
   incident 6/23). Correct that the breach-exit is the operator's call (THE LINE) — but if the
   operator is asleep/offline, an already-through position rides unprotected indefinitely
   while continuing to fall.
2. **update_stop is cancel-then-place** (`order_manager.py:925-980`): between the cancel and a
   double-failed place there is a genuinely-naked window; the loud alarm fires only after the
   3s retry also fails (#433). Bounded (seconds-to-minutes) but nonzero, and it runs daily on
   every trailed position.
3. **Day-1→GTC handoff chain**: the DAY child stop dies at the close; overnight protection
   depends on the 16:45 EOD `update_stop` → GTC replacement, backstopped by the 21:00 evening
   sync. Two-job failure (the 14-restart weekend class) = naked overnight, caught only next
   9:00 watchdog *if* the WS expiry event nulled `stop_order_id` (the watchdog predicate
   misses a stale-but-set pointer; `_ensure_stop_coverage` at boot/sync is the true catcher).

**Hardening (design only).** ⚖ For (1): a pre-committed operator standing rule for the breach
case — e.g. "if stop-above-market breach unacknowledged for N minutes during RTH, market-out"
— would be a *sell discipline* change: operator-only, surface at a sitting with the 6/23
incident data. For (3): a cheap invariant — an end-of-day assertion job (16:50) that every
`filled` live position has a **GTC** stop confirmed at broker (one `get_open_orders` sweep;
alert on any DAY-or-missing) — telemetry, no money. **PLAN refs:** #444 (adjacent sweep), #456(b).

## F6 — Sizing-input corruption: a bad ORB bar mints an oversized position

**Mechanism.** `shares = floor(risk_dollars / (orb_high − orb_low))`
(`order_manager.py:114-116`). `validate_orb_entry` has NO minimum-range floor — only
zero-range and >1.5×ATR-max checks (`backtester/filters.py:190-205`). A corrupted/thin first
bar (IEX feed default, `ALPACA_DATA_FEED=iex` — the calibration itself flags "IEX 1-min-ORB
entry geometry" as a caveat, safeguards.md:173-177) with a 5-cent range on a $20 name →
divide-by-tiny → clamped to the 20% cap → a 20%-of-equity position whose stop is 5 cents away:
functionally a coin-flip with guaranteed stop-out where the real loss is slippage at
multiples of the nominal risk. Symmetric feed risk: a wrong-side bar (bad high) sets the entry
trigger below the real breakout = adverse fills. Related upstream: `check_fade_guard` and bar
retries fail-soft on feed flakiness (deliberate, `entry_pipeline.py:103-110`).

**Current mitigation.** The 20% notional cap bounds the position; ATR-max rejects wide bars;
`_apply_composite_multiplier` clamps any multiplier at the builder baseline (RED-3,
`entry_pipeline.py:164-179`) so nothing sizes *past* the caps; the ORB OHLC is audit-logged
per entry (`orb_bar_fetched`, :400-408).

**Residual gap.** Nothing rejects a suspiciously TIGHT range, and per-share-risk realism isn't
checked against ATR on the low side (a stop 0.02×ATR away is noise, not structure).

**Hardening (design only).** ⚖ A minimum-range floor (e.g. `orb_range ≥ k×ATR`, k≈0.1-0.2, or
a min risk-per-share in bps of price) is a detection/entry-criterion change → SSoT
(`magna53_ep.md`) + backtest N≥10 + sign-off. Cheap no-money first step: backtest the
would-have-blocked cohort from historical `orb_bar_fetched` audit rows to size the class
before proposing the threshold. Also fold the VIX-staleness note here: sizing reads
*yesterday evening's* VIX (`mi_market_regime` row from the nightly pull; regime fetch
`live_tracker.py:307-311`) — a crash-morning VIX spike is unseen at 9:31; the regime-halving
(`qqq_ema_bullish is False → ×0.5`, `order_manager.py:111-112`) is also last-night's read.
**PLAN ref:** none exact (new finding; #456(a) is the adjacent-but-stale line — see corrections).

## F7 — Cap integrity: races and zombies around `OPEN_POSITION_STATUSES`

**Mechanism.** Three small holes in the 5-slot envelope
(`OPEN_POSITION_STATUSES = ('filled','order_placed','confirmed')`, per-mode count
`live_tracker.py:121-133`):
1. **Check→insert TOCTOU** (#461, filed): concurrent 9:31 entries can both pass the cap read
   before either inserts → cap+1. Semaphore(5) concurrency at `live_tracker.py:318` makes this
   real-if-rare.
2. **`submitting` is not an open status**: during `submit_entry`'s claim window
   (`order_manager.py:199-204`) the row leaves the counted set — a same-instant second
   candidate undercounts by one. Sub-second, compounds (1).
3. **Confirmed-zombie starvation**: the auto-enter path sets `status='confirmed'` *before*
   `submit_entry` (`entry_pipeline.py:510-515`); a crash/halt between those leaves a
   `confirmed` row with no broker order that **counts toward the cap forever** — no reaper
   covers it (`expire_stale_proposals` only expires `pending_confirmation`,
   `order_manager.py:2231+`; `cancel_unfilled_entries` only `order_placed`, :2280+). The #436
   phantom class, one status over. Loss mode = slot starvation: in a 25%-WR winner-driven
   strategy, silently losing 1-2 slots is a slow expectancy bleed (the #197 evidence: the
   cap-blocked cohort were the winners).

**Current mitigation.** #436 fork B removed the `pending_confirmation` phantoms; the halt-block
path leaving `confirmed` is deliberate (re-submittable after `/resume`); coverage_drift D3
events would eventually flag DB-vs-broker divergence.

**Residual gap.** (3) has no automatic reaper and its cap effect is invisible until a
`block:max_positions` skip fires on a good candidate.

**Hardening (design only).** #461 already carries the fix menu for (1)+(2) (atomic
count+insert). For (3): a stale-`confirmed` reaper mirroring `expire_stale_proposals`
(confirmed + `entry_order_id IS NULL` + proposed_at < today → `expired`) — same self-heal
shape, no live values touched, but it mutates trade-state rows → still ⚖ sign-off + tests.
**PLAN ref:** #461 (extend its DoD to cover the zombie, or file the sibling).

## F8 — Account-mode cross-contamination (largely closed; one legacy fallback pattern left)

**Mechanism.** The catastrophic version — paper-calibrated behavior firing real orders, or a
live event mutating paper state — is triple-gated by the dual-account invariants: mode-bound
COIDs at every submit (`make_client_order_id`, e.g. `order_manager.py:213`, `:943`),
cross-account event rejection pre-DB-mutation (`trade_stream._verify_event_account_mode`),
`account_mode` filter on every trade query (`docs/architecture/dual_account.md:36-39`).
Boot-time `assert_service_role_coherent` kills the double-execution topology
(`constants.py:118-157`).

**Residual gap.** The `trade.get("account_mode") or current_account_mode()` fallback pattern
(`order_manager.py:211`, `:883`, etc.): a live row that ever lost its `account_mode` would be
*managed* through the paper client (prod legacy env leaves `current_account_mode()='paper'`) —
stop updates would 404 against the wrong account and the real position would drift unmanaged.
No incident has occurred; the column is set at insert (`entry_pipeline.py:470-495`). The #444
sweep (label sites) is BUILT with residual sites explicitly listed (order_manager ×6,
live_tracker :400/:433, entry_pipeline:297) — those are labels, not routing, but the routing
fallbacks share the pattern. PDT itself: **retired with the rule** (FINRA 4210; R2 WITHDRAWN
7/11 on verification — stands; broker-side intraday-margin rejection is the fail-safe).

**Hardening.** Fold "flag `or current_account_mode()` ROUTING fallbacks on trade rows" into the
#444 verify-live residual pass — a NULL-mode trade row should fail LOUD, not route to paper.
**PLAN ref:** #444.
