## 0. The answer

**Yes, on the letter of the pre-registered bar — on ONE rung, in ONE cell family, carried by TWO names.**

- On `ep_low_reclaim` (buy = the 5-min close that reclaims the EP-day low), a **0.25×ADR stop with up to
  3 tries** made **+54.4R across 46 ex-May names** where **one try at 0.75×ADR made +5.3R on the same 46
  names** (trail exit, equal dollars risked per attempt). Worst single name: **−3.0R vs −1.0R** — exactly
  the two extra attempts' nominal risk, not more. It survives the pre-registered drop-best-campaign check
  (+18.2R after removing NRIX) and is positive in every readable month (June +12.9R, July +41.6R).
- **The cost side of the operator's trade-off, per name:** 22 of the 46 names hit −2R and **11 of 46 spent all
  three stops and never got positioned**; 25 of 46 never positioned at all. Under the −1.0R stop convention
  the worst name is −3R; **charging the 8 stops that gapped through at the open, the cell is +43.7R and the
  worst name is −5.8R** (AGX). At **live sizing** the 20% notional cap lets a ~1.5% stop deploy only a third
  of the risk budget, so in dollar-equivalent terms the cell is **+14.9R-eq vs +7.4R-eq** for the baseline —
  still ahead, by 2× rather than 10×.
- **What it rests on:** NRIX (+36.3R after two −1R cuts) and EFOR (+33.2R after one) are +69.5R of the +54.4R;
  the other 44 names net **−15.0R**. One drop survives (the pre-registered check); a second does not. The
  same-pattern-only re-entry variant does not survive even one drop (−1.8R).
- **The kill condition did not fire** — 2–3 tight tries do not merely reproduce one wider try's outcome; they
  catch the same winners (NRIX, EFOR, ABVX, SMCI) with a stop a third as wide, so the same move pays ~3× the R.
  But with 32 cells scanned on this rung under the primary policy (128 across the four rungs, plus 64
  sensitivity cells here) and the result on two names, its honest status is the same
  as the 09-01 grid's own cell: **a candidate for the forward out-of-sample read, not an edge.**
- **Elsewhere retries subtract:** on `ep_close_620_prox` every retry column is worse than one try (0.25×ADR ×3
  trail −21.6R vs +11.4R one try at 0.75); on `ep_close_reclaim` the one passing-looking cell is FET alone;
  `ep_high_break` has 9 readable ex-May names and cannot be read against an n≥30 bar.

## 1. The decision this serves, and the pre-registration

The operator's idea, verbatim: *"with tight stop losses, to get positioned often requires multiple tries, this
is the trade off — you take smaller cuts but more tries vs bigger cuts but less tries. So an idea here is that
we keep a very tight stop, like what you've listed, but we take more tries, which means if we get shaken out by
intraday volatility, we just retry let's say up to 3 times."*

**Pre-registered (design doc §7 Phase 1, unchanged):**
- **Question:** on the same 267 EPs, does a TIGHT stop with up to 3 tries beat ONE try at the 0.75–1.0×ADR
  stop — measured per NAME at equal dollar risk per attempt?
- **Grid:** stop ∈ {0.25, 0.50, 0.75, 1.00}×ADR · attempts ∈ {1, 2, 3, unlimited in the 20-session window} ·
  exit ∈ {M-none, M-trail} · rung `ep_low_reclaim` first, then the other three.
- **Outputs:** per-name total R · attempts fired · campaigns that spend every stop and never position · ≥4R
  campaigns · worst cumulative drawdown on one name · pooled AND ex-May · drop-best-campaign. August unreadable.
- **Pass bar:** a (stop × attempts) cell beats the 1-attempt 0.75×ADR cell on total R ex-May at n≥30 campaigns
  AND does not worsen the worst-name drawdown by more than the extra attempts' nominal risk.
- **Kill:** if 2–3 tight attempts merely reproduce one wider attempt's outcome at a worse drawdown, the retry
  idea is priced and closed.

**Declared in the probe's docstring BEFORE the run (the pre-registration left these open):**
1. **Primary re-entry policy = "either":** after a stop-out, the next attempt is whichever of the lane's two
   recorded re-entry shapes fires first — `same_pattern` (the rung's own pattern from a fresh state: another
   undercut of the EP low and a close back above it — the "same-trigger re-arm") or `new_high_break` (a break
   above the highest high since the EP). This is the faithful re-drive of the lane, which records both, and the
   policy most generous to catching. Single-shape policies are sensitivity only. Same-day ties take
   `same_pattern` (21 ties on this rung).
2. **A re-entry opens only after a STOP-OUT.** A trail exit or the 20-session time exit ends the campaign — a
   management exit is not "shaken out".
3. **The window is the lane's `REENTRY_WATCH_SESSIONS` = 20 trading sessions from each stop-out**, starting the
   session after it (the lane's own day-2+ boundary), chained: each stop-out opens a fresh window. The other
   reading — 20 sessions from the EP — gives the identical headline: **0 of the 45 re-entries in the headline
   cell fired more than 20 trading sessions after the EP** (all 46 names, +54.4R under either reading).
4. **Every attempt's stop = its own entry − k×ADR$**, the EP-anchored ADR$ fixed for the campaign (the #616
   variant basis — identical to how the lane stamps a re-entry row).
5. **Readability = chained maturity:** a campaign is readable under a cell only if every attempt in its chain is
   mature (20 post-fire sessions existed by 08-31) and every window searched with no fire was fully observable.
   The pass bar is judged on the **COMMON readable set** (readable under every stop × attempts × arm of the
   primary policy) so every cell is on the same names.

## 2. Method / population

- **Population:** the backfill's 602 recorded first-attempt fires over the 267 live-source `mi_ep_alerts`
  campaigns (May 74 · Jun 53 · Jul 41 · Aug 99; alerts 2026-05-01..08-31; bars `mi_daily_closes`
  02-15..08-31 + `mi_intraday_bars` 1-min for the backfill's needed pairs). `ep_low_reclaim` fired on 203 of
  them; the chain for each is built from that recorded first fire.
- **Instrument:** the lane's own pure functions (`delayed_entry_shadow.py`): `evaluate_session_minute/daily`,
  `evaluate_session_620`, `replay_level_break`, `compute_settlement` — the re-entry shapes mirror
  `_record_reentries_for` / `_replay_same_pattern_reclaim` / `_replay_same_pattern_620` line for line; nothing
  re-implemented. One chain per (rung × campaign × stop × shape policy × arm) with unlimited attempts; the
  1/2/3-attempt columns are prefixes of that chain, so attempts-fired and never-positioned counts fall out of
  the same rows.
- **Fidelity gates, both green before any number was read:** (1) the walk reproduces the recorded 602 fires
  exactly (0 entry/stop/date mismatches); (2) **the loop's attempts=1 leg reproduces the 09-01 stop grid's
  `adr_025/050/075/100` rows — all 4,816 rows, both arms, 0 drift.** The first leg IS the grid.
- **Equal dollar risk per attempt:** each attempt's R is in its own units (risk = entry − stop), so summing
  attempts sums equal-dollar bets. **Per-name total R is the unit of account; no per-trade average appears.**
- **Level-break attempts** (`new_high_break`, and `same_pattern` on the high-break rung) derive the touch bar
  from contiguous 5-min bars when present (the grid probe's convention); otherwise they settle daily-grade with
  the pessimistic day-0 stop-first rule. Count per cell shown (`day0-pess` in `_545rt_report.txt`): 0–3 on the
  capped `ep_low_reclaim` columns, 15–26 on unlimited.
- **A stop settles at −1.0R** (the house convention, as in every prior study); `mae_r` keeps the raw low. §4
  re-charges stops that gapped through at the open, so the convention cannot hide the tight stop's real cuts.
- **Maturity discipline inherited:** immature settled rows are stops by construction and never pooled.
  **August is unreadable** (readable ~late September); every number is May–July. Shown pooled AND ex-May.
- **⚠ A limitation the pre-registration did not foresee — blind sessions.** The 09-01 backfill pulled minute
  bars only inside each campaign's 20-session lane window. Chained re-entry windows run past it, and a
  fresh-state `same_pattern` walk that needs minutes there finds none → the session is **blind** (facts fold,
  no fire, counted — the abstain rule). Across every chain: 783 (ticker, day) pairs; `mi_intraday_bars` holds
  162 of them (uncaptured, pullable at $0 — `_545rt_blind_capture.sql`), 621 are not in the table at all (the
  live lane reads Polygon, so the lane is not blind there). A blind session can only HIDE a re-entry, never
  invent one. **On the headline cell it touches 4 of 46 ex-May names (9 sessions); on the unlimited column
  8 of 46 (39 sessions).** The headline does not turn on it; the unlimited column is understated in attempts
  both ways.
- **Common readable set on `ep_low_reclaim`: 99 campaigns pooled (May 53 · Jun 36 · Jul 10), 46 ex-May.**
  Of the ex-May campaigns not in it, 77 are censored (a re-entry window open at the horizon) and 18 abstain —
  the July EPs mostly cannot chain to maturity by 08-31. Each cell's own readable n (up to 130/70) is shown
  beside it; a pairwise read (readable under the cell AND the baseline, n=63–70 ex-May) is given for the key
  cells as a robustness check.

as wide), and on the names that do not, the cost is bounded at three cuts. That is precisely the trade-off the
operator described, and on this population it nets positive.

**What the number is made of, so it can be weighed honestly:**

## 8. What this does not answer

- **Whether the cell survives out of sample.** 46 names, two carriers, 32 primary cells scanned on this rung. Only the forward walk
  (or a pre-May backfill extension of the minute capture) separates a real tight-stop-retry edge from a
  well-dressed survivor. The 09-01 grid's cell has the same status; this one is thinner in names and fatter in R.
- **August, at all** — 99 campaigns, 0 readable under any chained cell; readable ~late September.
- **The 77 ex-May campaigns censored by the horizon** (mostly July EPs whose chains cannot mature by 08-31) —
  the common set is June-heavy (36 of 46). July is 10 names, two of which are EFOR and SMCI.
- **Blind sessions.** 783 (ticker, day) pairs the fresh walks needed minutes for and had none; 4 of the 46
  headline names touched (9 sessions), 8 of 46 on the unlimited column. Hidden re-entries are more likely
  extra cuts than extra catches (the median re-entry is a full stop), so the capped cells are probably
  overstated by a few R, not understated. `_545rt_blind_capture.sql` pulls the 162 pairs prod holds at $0;
  the other 621 would need Polygon.
- **Fill reality on a 1.2–1.6% stop.** The replay fills the reclaim bar's close and the stop at the level;
  a stop that narrow is one spread-and-slippage event on a $15 stock, and 8 of 70 stops gapped through at the
  open. The gap-adjusted column is a floor on that cost, not the whole of it.
- **Sizing.** Equal dollars per attempt is the pre-registered unit; the cap-scaled column shows what the
  live sizer would actually deploy. Whether a tight-stop lane should size differently is Axis 7 — named, out
  of scope, the operator's.
- **Same-day re-entry** — structurally excluded (the lane's day-2+ boundary, ruled 2026-08-30). The two
  carriers re-entered on the NEXT session; a same-day loop is a different, unmeasured cell.
- **Any exit beyond the two modelled arms** — the +2R partial is deliberately not an arm (operator 08-30).
  The trail arm's advantage here is again loss-truncation on the non-carriers, not exit alpha on the carriers
  (NRIX's MFE was +48R; the trail kept +38R).
- **Per-stock character** — one global ADR multiple, as everywhere in this program.
