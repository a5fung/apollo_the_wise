## 5. What the matcher does today (`agents/market_intelligence/tv_news_shadow.py`)

- `build_shadow_row` (367–460): same-day TradingView items (ET date = alert date, or prior
  trading day ≥ 16:00 ET) whose `normalize_title(title)` is not in the set of our normalised titles
  → `tv_items_we_missed`. `normalize_title` (234) lower-cases, strips punctuation, collapses
  whitespace. That is the whole rule.
- Its own docstring says it "deliberately does NOT dedupe across providers re-titling the same real
  story" and the DDL calls the column "an UPPER BOUND". PENG shows the bound is vacuous: 0 of 17
  retitles matched, so the upper bound equals the item count.
- There is NO time cutoff — `published` is compared to a date, never to when we captured our
  corpus. ACN shows that counts 25 of 25 by construction.
- The corpus it diffs against comes only from `mi_ep_catalyst_metrics.raw_*_news_json`
  (`get_catalyst_metrics_raw_corpus`, db.py:18543) — the earnings-branch table, §2.

## 7. Build spec — one Sonnet card, all telemetry, nothing touches a grade, score, admission or trade

🛑 THE LINE: every part below writes its own table or the shadow's; no grading/entry/sizing/
safeguard path reads any of it. Deploy scope `market-agent`, weekend window.

### Part 1 — the comparison frame (`tv_news_shadow.py::build_shadow_row`)
- `corpus` gains `captured_at` (TIMESTAMPTZ): `mi_ep_catalyst_metrics.extracted_at` or the new
  table's `captured_at` (Part 3). Stored on the row as `our_captured_at`.
- Same-day items fall into THREE buckets (new INT columns):
  `tv_items_before_grade` = `published <= captured_at` (we could have held it);
  `tv_items_in_repoll_window` = `captured_at < published <= 10:00:00 ET on alert_date`
  (`_TV_ACTIONABLE_CUTOFF_ET = time(10, 0)` — the scan's last tick and the 10:00 ET unfilled-cancel;
  this is the BFLY class: the release arrived AFTER the 07:01 grade and the cache pinned `routine`,
  which is what `should_repoll_shadow` (#344) exists for — the DoD's "same-day coverage we lacked"
  must include it or the task's founding case is un-flaggable);
  `tv_items_after_cutoff` = later (informational — "what the day's coverage became").
- `tv_coverage_reaches_grade_time = oldest_item_published <= captured_at`. When False the window
  has rolled past our grade time and cannot speak to the first two buckets (the ACN case: oldest
  09:31 ET vs capture 07:10).
- `tv_items_we_missed` is NULL when `corpus is None` **or** `tv_coverage_reaches_grade_time` is
  False — "cannot tell", never a list. Otherwise it is the items of the first two buckets that
  Part 2 leaves unmatched, each stored with its `bucket`.
- `tv_items_on_alert_date` unchanged.

### Part 2 — story-level matching (new pure function in `tv_news_shadow.py`)
`match_tv_item(tv_item, our_items, company_tokens) -> "title" | "story" | "class" | "move" | "none"`,
evaluated in that order; `our_items = [{title, published|None, src}]`.
- **Normalisation:** lower-case; keep digits and the decimal point inside a number and drop a
  trailing `.00` (`$1.00`→`1`, `$85.00`→`85`, `$566.685M`→`566.685m`, `68%`→`68`); strip other
  punctuation; drop STOPWORDS ∪ `company_tokens` ∪ the ticker. `company_tokens` is data-driven:
  tokens present in ≥ 50% of our titles (for PENG that yields {penguin, solutions}) — no
  company-name lookup needed. CLASS_WORDS (never an anchor) = every class keyword below plus
  `buy|sell|hold|overweight|underweight|outperform|neutral|raised|raises|lowers|cut|target|price`.
- **`title`:** existing `normalize_title` equality.
- **`story`:** Jaccard(content tokens) ≥ 0.5, OR Jaccard ≥ 0.25 AND ≥ 1 shared ANCHOR, where an
  anchor is a numeric token or an alphabetic token of ≥ 5 letters not in STOPWORDS ∪ CLASS_WORDS
  (the class keyword lists below). Card tunes the two thresholds; the fixture assertions decide.
- **`class`:** the TradingView title carries ≥ 1 CATALYST class and ≥ 1 of those classes is present
  among our SAME-DAY titles (our item passes `is_same_day_item` by its own `published`; an
  undated FMP item counts as held). Without that restriction a 7-day-old item of ours absorbs a
  fresh one — ACN's 09-28 Susquehanna PT would absorb the 10-01 consensus-PT item. The same
  same-day restriction applies to the `move` rule below. Classes and seed keywords (regex, word-bounded, case-insensitive; the card
  may extend, never remove):
  earnings `q[1-4]|[1-4]q|quarter|fy\d{2,4}|fiscal|earnings|eps|revenue|sales|results|guidance|
  outlook|profit|beats?|misses?|estimate|transcript` · analyst `price target|maintains?|maintained|
  reiterates?|upgrades?|downgrades?|initiates?|rating` · exec_change `appoints?|names?|hires?|
  resigns?|steps down|cfo|ceo|coo|cto|chief .{0,20}officer` · mna `acquire[sd]?|acquisition|
  merger|buyout|takeover|to buy|definitive agreement` · deal `partnership|partners? with|
  collaborat|agreement|contract|awarded?|selected by` · financing `offering|priced|private
  placement|convertible|notes due|at-the-market|warrants?|dilut` · regulatory `fda|approv|
  clearance|510\(k\)|phase [1-3]|trial|topline|pdufa` · legal `lawsuit|class action|
  investigation|settle|probe` · product `launch|unveils?|introduces?`.
  `commentary` (NOT a catalyst class) `why|here is why|here's why|what's going on|trades? up|
  trading higher|jumps?|surges?|soars?|rallies|pops?|climbs?|rising|best day|52-week|
  (month|year) high|premarket|pre-market|overnight|midday|market talk`. Anything else → `other`.
- **`move`:** the TradingView item is commentary-only (no catalyst class) AND our corpus holds ≥ 1
  catalyst-class item — we held a reason for the move it describes. (BFLY's corpus holds none,
  so its commentary piece is NOT absorbed by this rule.)
- **`none`:** everything else — counted in `tv_items_we_missed`, each item stored as
  `{"title","provider","published","class"}` so a reader can discount `other`/`commentary`.
- **Analyst guard, stated as a fixture not a special case:** Rosenblatt $100 vs our Stifel $85
  share only class words (maintains, buy, price, target) → no anchor → `class`, never `story`.
- New JSONB column `tv_match_summary` = `{"title": n, "story": n, "class": n, "move": n, "none": n}`
  over the items of the first two buckets (before grade + re-poll window).
