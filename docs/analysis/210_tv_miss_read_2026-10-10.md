## 2. Why 7 of 10 rows are unreadable

- All 7 have **no `mi_ep_catalyst_metrics` row at all** (q1_read.out §Q2: `metrics_row = f`, every
  `raw_*` column NULL because there is no row to hold them). It is not a NULL column inside a row.
- That table is written by ONE path: `persist_catalyst_metrics` inside the earnings-extraction branch
  of `ep_detector.py` (~5405): `EARNINGS_REVENUE_GATE_ENABLED and (earnings_today_match or Claude's
  text signals earnings) and revenue_stage and catalyst_quality in ("strong", "game_changer")`.
  ERO, ACN and PENG are earnings names and took that branch (rows at 09:55, 07:10, 09:35 ET); the
  other 7 (a conference, Bitcoin, Brazil's election ×3, a sympathy move, a sector rally) never could.
- Across all alerts since 2026-09-01, **5 of 32 (16%) have a metrics row.** So the shadow can only
  ever read earnings-day alerts, and only the subset the raw grade called routine or
  no-direct-source. That is the structural cause of "0.6 readable rows a week".
- The shadow's reader (`get_catalyst_metrics_raw_corpus`) correctly returns `None` for a missing
  row and the writer correctly stores NULL (the 09-08 fix held: 7 NULLs, 0 false `[]`).

## 5. What the matcher does today (`agents/market_intelligence/tv_news_shadow.py`)

- `build_shadow_row` (367–460): same-day TradingView items (ET date = alert date, or prior
  trading day ≥ 16:00 ET) whose `normalize_title(title)` is not in the set of our normalised titles
  → `tv_items_we_missed`. `normalize_title` (234) lower-cases, strips punctuation, collapses
  whitespace. That is the whole rule.
- Its own docstring says it "deliberately does NOT dedupe across providers re-titling the same real
  story" and the DDL calls the column "an UPPER BOUND". PENG shows the bound is vacuous: 0 of the 17
  seen items matched (11 retitles and 6 commentary pieces), so the upper bound equals the item count.
- There is NO time cutoff — `published` is compared to a date, never to when we captured our
  corpus. ACN shows that counts 25 of 25 by construction.
- The "prior trading day" comes from `shared.dates.last_trading_day`, which is weekend-only by its
  own docstring: for ERO 2026-09-08 (the Tuesday after Labor Day) the same-day period starts 16:00
  on Monday 09-07, a day the market was shut (harmless there — ERO's oldest item was 07-06 — wrong
  in general).
- The corpus it diffs against comes only from `mi_ep_catalyst_metrics.raw_*_news_json`
  (`get_catalyst_metrics_raw_corpus`, db.py:18543) — the earnings-branch table, §2.

## 7. The matcher, redesigned anchor-first and PROVEN (`scripts/probes/_wk1010_210/tv_story_matcher.py`)

**Why not a similarity threshold.** The review showed no Jaccard threshold satisfies the fixture:
"Q4 Earnings Call Highlights" vs our transcript MUST match and scores 0.33–0.43 with no shared
anchor; Rosenblatt $100 vs Stifel $85 must NOT match and scores 0.40; and the analyst guard breaks
on the Benzinga template ("Rosenblatt Maintains Buy…, Raises Price Target"). So the match is decided
by WHAT is shared, not how much — a stated rule per verdict, evaluated in this order:

| verdict | rule | fixture pair that pins it |
|---|---|---|
| `title` | `normalize_title` equality (today's rule, kept first) | BFLY filler item ↔ the same Polygon title |
| `story` | same NON-earnings catalyst class on both sides AND a shared anchor (a $ / % / FY figure, or an alphabetic token ≥ 4 letters outside stop/class/generic/company words); for `analyst` the anchor must be the FIRM, extracted from the capitalised run before an analyst verb or after by/at/from; two items both carrying $ figures that share none never match | PENG Cumming ×3 ↔ our CFO wire; Needham ×2 ↔ our Needham; Dow Jones "…$85.00 From $75.00 by Stifel" ↔ our Stifel; Needham $85 ↔ Stifel $85 → not story |
| `event` | an earnings-class item on a day we hold an earnings RESULTS item (not a preview) — one company reports once a day | "Q4 Earnings Call Highlights" ↔ our transcript; ACN Reuters results summary ↔ our Q4 wire |
| `move` | a commentary-only title on a day we hold any non-preview catalyst item | "Why Is PENG Stock Rising…"; Barron's "Memory Business Soars" |
| `class` | same class among our same-day items, no anchor proves the same story — a second analyst, a second contract, actual results vs our preview | Rosenblatt ↔ Stifel; ACN_PREVIEW: Reuters results ↔ our "Set to Report Q4" preview |
| `none` | nothing above | BFLY Business Wire release; ACN TradingView consensus PT |

**Two stored lists** (correction 2 and the 09-08 NULL rule together): `tv_items_unmatched_seen` =
the `none` and `class` items of the before-grade and re-poll buckets, written whenever a diff is
possible, whatever the window's reach; `tv_items_we_missed` (the DoD column) = that list when the
window reaches the START of the same-day period (oldest item ≤ 16:00 ET on the prior NYSE trading
day), else NULL. `none` (strict) and `none + class` (loose) are reported separately (correction 6).
