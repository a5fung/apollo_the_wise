## Method

- **$0, stored data only.** Two read-only SELECT captures from prod, each run once. No Polygon, no Alpaca, no model calls.
- **Replay engine:** `scripts/ep_replay.py` (`walk_campaign`, rule-set `current` = era D: stop `2·ORB_low − ORB_high`, +8R partial, breakeven armed at +3R on price, max(SMA10, SMA20) trail), fed this capture's minute and daily bars with `LAST_SETTLED` overridden to 2026-10-09. Every crosser is replayed **from the tick it crossed** (never earlier than 09:31, so the 09:30 tick enters from 09:31), ORB from the 09:30 bar, stop-buy at the ORB high. Abstains are first-class output, never filled in. **Tail counts** (best price reached) stop at the exit: a stop-out on day 0 ends the count at the exit minute, a later one at the exit session; the fill bar's own high counts.
- **Two entry mechanics for the in-window class.** The harness models the 09:31 stop-limit. Live, a name already above the ORB high at submission gets a LIMIT at last×1.002, refused when `(limit − stop) > 1.5 × (ORB_high − stop)` (#500 chase cap) — the path IONQ (cancelled) and PENG (filled) actually took. Section (a-live) adds that mechanic with the submit-minute open standing in for the last trade.
- **R frames, stated once.** *stop-R* = pnl ÷ (shares × (entry − placed stop)) — the risk he sizes on; every mean below is stop-R. For real trades the placed stop is `hard_stop` (the stop at entry). `stop_price` is wrong for this: it is the trailed live stop on names that armed breakeven (SEI, VICR, KOD). `risk_dollars` is also wrong: it is the sizing-time plan and differs from shares × (entry − placed stop) (PHVS $12.35 planned against $10.50). For replays it is the replay's own entry and stop. *ORB-R* = (price − entry) ÷ (entry − ORB low) — the frame the +8R partial and +3R breakeven are set in (`profit_target_r_per_share`); tail counts are given in both.
- **Calibration against the real book (harness contract):** the 5 closed era-D trades replayed with their stored ORB / ATR / shares — **3 of 5 within 0.25R** (PHVS −1.02 live / −1.00 replay; DFTX −1.01 / −1.00; CEG −1.00 / −1.00). VICR is +7.30 live / +7.01 replay (0.29 apart, same sign and size); SEI is +0.41 live against 0.00 replay (the live trail stop sat higher). Open marks agree in sign (KOD +6.66 real / +6.94 replay; HUM −0.59 / −0.61). The stop-limit model disagrees on both in-window HIGH alerts (IONQ: live cancelled on the chase cap, replay −1.00R; PENG: live filled at $72.21, replay no fill) — which is why (a-live) exists.
- Arithmetic: `scripts/probes/_wk1010_559/s1_population.py`, `s1b_gates.py`, `s2_replay.py` → `s2_replay_out.txt`, `s2_rows.tsv` (one row per replayed campaign); the lookups behind §5, §6 and the event-rate comparison are in `s3_corrections.py` → `s3_corrections_out.txt`.
