## 3. What the detector catches today — 59 scan days, 07-17 → 10-09

**Per day (median; n = 59):** ~545 names scanned · WATCH **5** (1–14) · TIGHTENING **1** (mean 1.7; 10 days

**Distinct names in 60 days:** 66 reached WATCH · 29 TIGHTENING · 12 COILED · 4 TRIGGERED (CHYM 08-19,
WDAY 08-28, XHLD 09-09, IOVA 09-14). **5 of 29 TIGHTENING names ever reached COILED; 4 of 12 COILED reached
TRIGGERED.**

**How long names stay:** TIGHTENING median 2 scan days (max 6) · COILED median 2.5 (max 8) · WATCH median 2
(max 9). The day after a TIGHTENING row: TIGHTENING 61, INVALIDATED 25, **7 × `base_age_0` with the pivot
moved UP (the breakout wiped the flag)**, COILED 4, the ADR liquidity floor 2, another base-age reject 2, the M&A filter 1 (n = 102 rows).

**Why 97% of names never reach WATCH (era C, 9 days, ~490 names/night):** ADR < 4% **41%** · run-up < 90%
**25%** · ADV < 500k **18%** · base under 3 days old **13%** · a close under the base low / 20-day / 50-day (INVALIDATED) **2%** · everything else (pole not near highs, MA
stack, flag depth, grinder) **~1%**. The liquidity floors take 59% of the scanned universe before any
geometry is read — measured and ruled kept 09-16; recorded here as the shape of the funnel, not a question.

- **2 rejected by another check that day** — NEO (moving-average stack), PURR (flag depth on the low).

So the COILED prerequisite alone blocked **3 of 15; the volume test blocked twice as many.** The earlier draft's "a
breakout can only register through COILED" counted any close over the first day's base high with no volume test, and
credited CDNA's September close, which came on 0.83x volume.
