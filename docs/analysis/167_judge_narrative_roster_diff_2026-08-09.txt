=== Conclusion ===
Zero HIGH crossings from the roster channel on this corpus, zero cases where the
judge credited the narrative axis to a roster/theme it doesn't actually match
(including the two cases running BOTH channels simultaneously), and the guardrail
case (S11) held under the combined-channel stress test. The channel is not proven
safe in general (this corpus has no case whose story genuinely SHOULD join the
roster — it can only show the judge doesn't falsely light on non-matches, which is
the risk-relevant direction: inflation), but on the actual real v2 roster shape
(12 members, 7-week-carried, real names/thesis) it produced no inflation and no
reachable-HIGH risk.

NOISE FLOOR (read part 1 and part 2 together, not in isolation): part 2 moved 7 of
33 comparable cases (2 up, 5 down) with narrative axis_read lit=False on every single
mover — i.e. this movement is pure call-to-call sampling variance between two
independently-sampled judge calls, not a roster effect. That 7/33 wobble, roughly
balanced up/down, is the measurement instrument's noise floor. Part 1 (the boolean
channel, gate-2-part-1) moved 4 of 33 — smaller than this noise floor in magnitude,
but ALL 4 were up and ZERO were down, a one-sided pattern the balanced part-2 noise
does not show. Read correctly: part 1's signal sits AT this instrument's resolution
limit, not below it — it is weak evidence of a small, real, one-directional (upward)
push from the boolean channel, not proof of nothing. Neither channel's signal is
large enough to separate cleanly from noise at n=33; both are small enough that
their one observed HIGH-adjacent event (part 1's S19 crossing) being blocked
upstream by the M&A filter matters a great deal to the overall risk read.
