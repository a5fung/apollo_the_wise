## What it means
- **TradingView adds a catalyst we lacked on about 1 alert in 27 fully read (~4%, n = 27); 1 in 36 overall, with 9 partly unseen.** The one it found (BSP) we graded routine on
  the raw grade; the acting grade was strong through the tier lattice, so the alert still fired.
- The matcher is **too strict on reworded stories** (RRC, GMAB, LRCX): the card's own unintended-consequence watch
  ("more than half of before-grade items 'none' where we held the same story") fired. That inflates the mechanical
  share; it changes no grade, because the shadow is telemetry only.
- The re-poll window (news arriving after our grade but before 10:00 ET) had a `none` item on 8 rows — e.g. IRON's
  trial result, the LSCC analyst raises. That is the re-grade-later question, a separate one from this DoD.
- Any change to the grading path that comes out of this (e.g. reading TradingView at grade time) is a
  CHANGE_PROCESS item with his sign-off. One real miss in 27 is not evidence for one.

## What this does not answer
- **Whether our catalyst READ is accurate.** This measures coverage (did a story exist that we never held), not
  whether we read a held story right — the HOOD 09-03 case (we held the label, missed the substance) is invisible here.
- **The 9 rolled rows' unseen stretch** (5 to 932 minutes after the prior close): a miss there cannot be ruled out.
- **Rates finer than ~1 in 27.** One confirmed miss gives no stable rate; it says TradingView rarely adds a catalyst,
  not how rarely.
- **Anything about returns or trading.** No grade, alert or trade path reads this table.
- **Stories neither source carried** (BFLY 06-18's partner-originated release reached neither feed set in time).
