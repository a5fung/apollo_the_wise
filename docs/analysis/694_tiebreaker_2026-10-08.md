# #694 — pre-open shortlist tie-breaker test (2026-10-08)

**The answer.** On stored data, the choice of tie-breaker does not decide any of his real EPs on any crowded morning. There were **14** mornings with more than 20 names left at the last check before the open (not 2 — the shortlist record only began 08-22). On every one of them, fix (b)'s own score already puts every real EP present (n=5: UMC and ARM 05-06, SNOW 05-07, PLTR 08-04, TEAM 08-07) above the tied group at the cut. So every tie-breaker keeps all 5. If today's live order (alphabetical within a theme bucket) were applied to those mornings, it would cut UMC and SNOW, keeping 3 of the 5 (PLTR and TEAM fall past 20 at the last check but sit inside the top 20 at earlier checks those mornings, which is when they would be graded). The gap order that actually ran then cut SNOW only. Taken alone, **20-day average dollar volume (the signed key) puts his real EPs highest**: it ranks a real EP above a same-morning pool-mate in 87% of 257 pairs (n=10 real EPs), and pre-market dollar volume is next at 82%. Counting each real EP once, the keys bunch together: dollar volume 0.78, pre-market dollar volume 0.74, gap 0.72, pre-market volume vs normal 0.70, RS 0.68. Against a random key (0.34-0.66 each-counted-once, 0.31-0.68 pair-weighted), zones (0.51) is inside the coin-flip range either way. RS sits just on the edge, and pair-weighted, gap, RS and pre-market volume vs normal are inside the range too. The one labelled name a tie-breaker ever decided is **BLZE on 08-04** (a GOOD chart, which leans the odds and never decides). The signed volume key and the zones key cut it; every other key keeps it. **Recommendation: keep the signed key (continuous 20-day dollar volume).** Nothing here argues for a change on his real EPs. With 10 real EPs, the data cannot rank the keys more finely than "the volume keys are at least as good as the rest".

**The one fork for him: inside the no-record group, the signed key falls back to A→Z.** Names with no volume record last night all score 32.5 and carry a volume of 0 in the tie-break. When the cut lands among them, the signed key orders them alphabetically, which is the bug being fixed, on a smaller scale. 07-30 shows it: 6 no-record names tied at the cut for 3 slots, and fix (b) cut the alphabetically last three (SMHI, SMTI, STGW; BOOM, CMCO and PN kept). This group is growing. The score universe shrank from ~3,900 to ~2,400 names on 06-16, and crowded boards went from 0-4 no-record names in May to 8-10 in Aug/Oct (10 of 25 on 10-05). Pre-market dollar volume is the only tested key logged for every name, so it is the only one that can order names inside that group (stored data is weaker than live here: 86 of 1,147 rows read exactly 0, all before 08-29, when the value is rebuilt from a rounded figure; live, it is logged directly). Build note: the sort runs before the real-time volume swap, so live it would order on the 15-minute-delayed pre-market volume unless the build reads real-time volume before the sort. Option 1: the signed key as-is. Option 2: the signed key, with pre-market dollar volume deciding among no-record names. Rec: option 2. It changes nothing for any real EP on record and removes the last alphabetical decision. His call.

Two findings sit outside the tie-breaker. They change what the build should cover, and both go to him (neither has been acted on):
- **The volume lookup is empty all day, not just before the open.** Both callers pass no date (`scheduler.py:1200`, `agent.py:783`), so `prev_date` falls back to today, and today's `mi_stock_scores` rows are written around 17:00. - **Under fix (b), real EPs with small dollar volume rank below names with no volume record at all.** 3 of the 10 real EPs (QURE, BFLY, HTFL) trade under $50M a day and score 10 out of 65. ## Method and population

**Population: `mi_ep_scan_log`, 2026-05-01..2026-10-08, n=1,147 rows on 111 market days.** Every row that reached the shortlist sort (`rank_by_gap IS NOT NULL`, which the scan assigns to every candidate right before the cut) at each day's **last pre-open check**. That is the latest `scan_time_et` before 09:30 ET among ranked rows (`q03_pool.sql`).
- That check is **09:25** on 107 days. It is earlier on four days, because the scanner ran no later pre-open check: 05-06 at 07:10, and 05-04, 05-08 and 05-13 at 07:55. The 10-05 cut flagged at 09:20 was re-checked here at 09:25 (25 names; 24 at 09:20).
- The pool has a median of 7 names a day and a maximum of 54. **14 days have more than 20 names:** 05-05, 05-06, 05-07, 05-08, 05-11, 05-13, 05-14, 05-26, 05-28, 07-30, 08-04, 08-05, 08-07 and 10-05.
- **09-17 is not a crowded pre-open morning:** 11 names at 09:25. Its crowd formed after the open.
- **April 13-30 is out.** Those rows carry no check time and no rank, and each name was logged once a day (n=661 rows, `q05_april_rows.out`), so no board can be rebuilt.
- Before 2026-08-22 the cut was gap-ordered; after that it was the collapsed alphabetical order. Neither acting order is what is tested here. Every stored pool is re-ranked under each key.
- Sanity gates (`results.txt`): the logged gap rank is reproduced from gap % on 1,139 of 1,147 rows (the 8 misses are exact ties in the rounded logged gap). The logged live shortlist rank (since 08-22) is reproduced **191 of 191** by "volume empty, theme first, then A→Z". So the live pre-open order really is alphabetical within a theme bucket. On 10-05 the fix-(b) cut here (SBS PAGS INTR GRML VIV) matches the 10-05 probe's would-add/would-drop at 09:25.

Theme flag (for the score): the logged `in_active_theme` from 08-29. Before that, it is rebuilt from `mi_themes`: for each theme, the latest snapshot dated 7 days to 1 day before the scan date, counted when that snapshot is not Retired and its stage is Accelerating or Mainstream. On the 191 rows that carry both, the rebuilt flag matches the logged one **191 of 191**.

**Orderings tested.** All use the real `ep_rubric.shortlist_prescore` / `shortlist_sort_key`.
- **A**: today's live order (volume empty → 65 or 32.5, then A→Z).
- **B**: fix (b) as signed (volume populated, tie-break on continuous volume, then A→Z).
- **T:key**: fix (b) score, then that key (missing ranks last), then A→Z.
- **S:key**: the key alone.

**Measures.**
- *Crowded mornings*: the literal list of names ranked past 20.
- *Separation*: within each day, every pair of (labelled name, unlabelled pool-mate), scored as the share of pairs where the labelled name ranks higher (ties count half). For BAD charts, lower is better. "All" ranks a missing value last; "present" keeps only pairs where both names have the key.
- *Noise band*: 2,000 random within-day orderings of the same pools.

Pair counts are dominated by the two 51- and 54-name May mornings: 59% of the 257 real-EP pairs come from 05-06 and 05-07. So an equal-weight reading, with each real EP counted once, is reported beside the pair-weighted one.

## What this does not answer

- **Thin sample.** 10 real EPs on 9 days, and only 5 of them on crowded mornings. 59% of the pairs come from two May mornings. A random key reaches 0.31-0.68 on this sample, so differences of a few points between keys mean nothing. No significance claims are made.
- **No outcome replay.** It does not say whether a name moved into the top 20 would have alerted, been bought, or paid. It measures only where his labelled names rank.
- **Only the last pre-open check is tested.** Names that join after 09:25 (ABNB 08-07, the 09-17 crowd) and every check after the open are not tested, even though the empty volume lookup affects them too.
- **Seven real EPs cannot be ranked by any key.** Four are from April (no board on record), and TDIC, ABNB and PENG never reached a pre-open board.
- **Pre-market volume vs normal is not tested fairly as a tie-breaker.** It is missing for exactly the names a tie-breaker acts on (cut names are never graded), and it exists only for about the top 500 names by volume. Using it would need a new lookup for every name at sort time, and its missing pattern is itself a size proxy.
- **Zones are computed on 19 days only** (n=457 rows), priced at the check price. The GOOD/OKISH and BAD readings for zones rest on 2 and 4 names.
- **Pre-market dollar volume before 08-29 is derived**, not logged, and is coarse when pre-market volume is tiny. After the real-time volume switch, graded names carry real-time volume while cut names carry the 15-minute-delayed feed.
- **The theme flag before 08-29 is rebuilt** from snapshots (matches the logged flag on all 191 rows from 08-29 that carry it; cannot be checked before that).
- **The score's weights, and the 32.5 given to names with no volume record, are not tested here.** That is the signed 08-22 design; the HTFL/QURE/BFLY observation above is surfaced, not acted on.
- **Boards differ by era.** 9 of the 14 crowded mornings are from May. Back then the gap floor was 8% (until 05-17), the score universe held ~3,900 names, and no universe-floor rows were logged before 08-24. Today's scan would not produce the same boards. The real-EP finding holds, because those names rank on volume either way, but the 87 decided slots and the May cut lists describe boards from older rules.
- **The chart sets are mostly small names**, so any size-based key ranks good and bad charts alike low (fix (b) separation: GOOD/OKISH 0.26, BAD 0.37). Charts lean the odds; they do not separate the keys here.
