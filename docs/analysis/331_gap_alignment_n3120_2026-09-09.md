# #331 — gap-vs-structure alignment at N=3,120: the axis is wrong in SIGN, and it is not a size artifact

**2026-09-09 · read-only · $0 · cohort `mi_theme_axis_shadow` (3,120 rows, 3,051 computable, 2,441
with a prior close) · probes `scripts/probes/_331_gap_alignment_evidence.py` and
`scripts/probes/_331_price_confound.py`**

## Method / population — which rows, over what window

- ⚠ **Two populations, and only one of them decides.** The DECIDING cut is **our EP alerts**
  (N=299 settled, 171 scoring ≥70) — the names a meta-rubric axis would actually grade. The wider
  `mi_theme_axis_shadow` cohort is context only: **only 347 of its 3,120 rows are EP alerts**, so
  89% of it is outside the axis's reach. The first version of this document led with the wide
  cohort; that was a population error of exactly the kind §2 of the standard warns about.
- **Rows.** Every row of `mi_theme_axis_shadow` (**N=3,120**, all of it, no date filter), left-joined
  to `mi_ep_scan_outcomes` for forward returns and `mi_market_regime` for the regime label.
  **3,051** carry a settled `fwd_5d_pct`; **2,441** additionally have a prior daily close and form
  the price-band control.
- **How the classes are derived.** Not stored — recomputed from `mi_daily_closes` bars by
  `_331_gap_alignment_evidence.classify()`, comparing the alert-day OPEN against the 15-day base
  high and the full trailing high. The control probe imports that same function, so the two runs
  cannot disagree about what a class is.
- **Era, and the honest warning about it.** The cohort spans **2026-03-24 → 2026-09-08** (queried, not recalled — I first wrote 04-27 from memory and it was wrong), which
  contains real rule changes (gap floor 10→9, the 2R stop, real-time admission, rubric v4). The
  admission filter therefore VARIES across these rows. That is tolerable here only because the
  question is about a **bar-derived geometric property of the gap**, computed from raw daily bars
  and independent of which filter admitted the name — but it would NOT be tolerable for any claim
  about hit rate, yield or tradeability, and no such claim is made below.
- **What was NOT used.** No `mi_live_trades` rows, so nothing here is the output of the rules that
  were live on a given day. No paper/live pooling question arises because no trade rows are read.
- **Cost: $0.** Two read-only probes against prod, each captured to a file on its first run.

## The proposal this tests

Credit a gap that **punches through** prior resistance (institutional confirmation) and withhold
credit from one that **fades into congestion**. Three classes, computed from bars only:

| class | definition |
|---|---|
| `punch_through` | the open clears the **trailing high** of all prior bars |
| `clears_base_near_miss` | the open clears the **15-day base high** but not the trailing high |
| `fades_into_congestion` | the open clears neither |

## ⚠ CORRECTION, same day — the first version of this document measured the wrong population

The operator asked *"what about our EPs"* and he was right to. `mi_theme_axis_shadow` is **not our
alert book**: only **347 of its 3,120 rows** are names we actually alerted on. **89% of the cohort
below is names the EP stack never surfaced**, and a meta-rubric axis grades EP candidates. The
whole-cohort numbers answer a question nobody asked.

**Re-cut on the population the axis would act on** (`scripts/probes/_331_ep_only.py`):

| our EP alerts only (N=299) | n | fwd-5d avg | median | win ≥5% |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| punch_through | 30 | 9.6% | 6.6% | 56% |
| clears_base_near_miss | 158 | 8.7% | 6.9% | 54% |
| fades_into_congestion | 111 | **12.8%** | **8.1%** | **72%** |

| EP alerts scoring ≥70 — the ones that can actually trade (N=171) | n | fwd-5d avg | median | win ≥5% |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| punch_through | 20 | 7.7% | 6.6% | 60% |
| fades_into_congestion | 60 | **13.0%** | **8.2%** | **76%** |

**The conclusion does not change — it gets stronger, and on the better evidence.** On our own EPs
the win-rate gap is **72% vs 56%** (and **76% vs 60%** on tradeable HIGHs) where the whole-cohort
gap was 63% vs 59%. **Win rate is the least size-biased of the three statistics**, so a gap that
widens on it is harder to dismiss than one that lives in the averages. At 10 days on our EPs it is
**85% vs 70%**.

⚠ **`punch_through` is n=30 on our EPs and n=20 on tradeable HIGHs** — under the N≥30 bar in one
case and at it in the other. That is the real limit on this result, and it is a limit on the class
the axis wanted to CREDIT.

## The result on the whole shadow cohort (kept for context, NOT the deciding evidence)

Forward 5-day, whole cohort (N=3,051):

| class | n | avg | median | win ≥5% |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| punch_through | 214 | **8.6%** | 6.3% | 59% |
| clears_base_near_miss | 721 | 9.9% | 5.5% | 53% |
| fades_into_congestion | 1,506 | **14.6%** | 7.7% | 63% |

`fades_into_congestion` beats `punch_through` on **average, median AND win rate**. It repeats at
fwd-10d (19.6% vs 11.9%), in **every regime** (Bull, Choppy, Correcting, Crisis), and with tape junk
excluded (11.8% vs 8.7%). This is not a marginal null — the axis credits the **worse** class.

## The confound I expected, and why it does not explain it

Forward **percent** return is size-biased, and the fades winners looked like it: VEEE +951%, SDOT
+394%, JLHL +383%, against a best of DELL +59% for punch_through. The class mix is indeed
price-dependent — `punch_through` is **2%** of $5–15 names and **18%** of names ≥$50.

**But the inversion survives stratification by prior close:**

| price band | n | punch_through | fades_into_congestion |
|---|---|---|---|
| $5–15 | 1,119 | 11.0% avg · 7.8% med | **17.7% avg · 8.5% med** |
| $15–50 | 706 | 8.3% avg · 6.2% med | **11.8% avg · 6.9% med** |
| ≥$50 | 616 | 8.1% avg · 5.8% med | **10.7% avg · 6.5% med** |

Fades wins on average and median in **all three bands**. The size explanation is dead; the effect is
real in the data as measured.

## Recommendation: DO NOT SHIP — and do NOT flip the sign either

- **Do not ship as designed.** Crediting `punch_through` would systematically penalise the
  better-performing class. The 2026-07-21 STEP-0 read (punch_through fails magnitude-independence,
  fades is a fat-tailed barbell) is confirmed and strengthened at 5× the sample.
- **Do not invert it.** Forward drift is **not** our P&L. Every number here is forward %-return over
  a calendar window; we enter on an ORB stop-buy and exit on a stop. A name that gaps into congestion
  is by construction choppier, so a larger share of that drift sits on the far side of a stop-out.
  Crediting `fades_into_congestion` on this evidence would repeat the error this analysis just
  caught — trusting a number that does not measure the thing we get paid on.
  [[prove-two-numbers-measure-the-same-thing]] [[measure-the-tail-not-the-median]]
- **The one measurement that would settle it: realized R through the actual bracket**, per class,
  replayed from stored minute bars under current rules. Until that exists, the axis is unproven in
  both directions and ships in neither. [[replay-raw-bars-not-old-trade-rows]]

## What this does not answer

- `punch_through` in the $5–15 band is **n=29** — under the probe's own N≥30 bar.
- The classes are heavily unbalanced (214 / 721 / 1,506); the rare class is the one being credited.
- Forward return is measured on a calendar window with no stop, no slippage and no ORB gate.
- **It does not answer whether either class is TRADEABLE.** Forward %-return over a calendar
  window is not realized R through an ORB entry and a stop; the two have never been reconciled on
  this cohort, and this document does not reconcile them.
- **`punch_through` is thin where it counts** — n=30 on our EPs, n=20 on tradeable HIGHs. The
  credited class is the under-powered one, and no amount of cohort width fixes that.
- **It does not answer whether the 2026-04→09 rule changes moved the classes themselves.** The cut
  is not split by era, so a within-era reversal would be invisible here.
- ⚖ **Nothing here changes a live grade.** Not shipping is the status quo and needs no sign-off;
  any future flip is ADR 0033 CHANGE_PROCESS + operator sign-off.
