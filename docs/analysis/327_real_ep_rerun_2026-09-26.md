## The decision it serves

Whether the delayed-entry lane's four patterns, applied to the EPs the system actually caught, give an entry or
a stop × target × exit / management shape that **catches big winners while limiting losses** (his frame,
2026-09-26) — and whether that answer holds across the two EP-rule eras. **What would change it:** a cell that
clears the pre-registered bar in ERA A and is confirmed in direction by ERA B; or an entry edge over the matched
control. **What would make it wrong:** a population error (the one that voided today's earlier docs — hence the
gate first), a coverage hole read as a null (hence the ERA B handling below), or a stop that sits inside the
instrument's noise read as "the entry is bad" (hence the grid).

**Checkpoint.** K = 10 sessions after the fire is primary (every campaign has ≥ 10 later sessions by 09-25); the
cell population is fires with ≥ K sessions elapsed, never settlement status; open positions are marked at the
session-K close. K = 20 is reported beside it and is an ERA A-only read (no ERA B fire reaches it). Width floor:
a fire whose stop is under 0.5% wide in the cell's own units is counted, not scored (Block 5's rule).

**A. Entry edge** per pattern vs a matched same-window non-fire control (Block 5 P2's walker, imported): fire at
its recorded entry, stop = entry − 1×ADR$, target = entry + 2×ADR$, first passage over day 0 (post-fire 5-min
bars; a level fire folds its whole day) + sessions 1..10, both straddle bounds (pess = stop first on a bar holding
both; opt = target first). Control = every non-fire session inside the 20-session window of each campaign where
that pattern fired, entered at the close, same clock. Beside it: the fire entered at the fire session's close
(like-for-like) and the fillable entry (max(level, open) for level fires). Gap in points, n both sides,
drop-best-name, per era.

imported): partial 1/3 at +2R/+3R × breakeven at +1R/+2R/after the partial × remainder on SMA10 / SMA20 / held to

`stack_walk_inputs` → `walk_arm(harvest="live_ladder")`, never re-implemented) in P6's two declared ORB-R frames

**C. Pass bar per cell, tail first, judged in ERA A:** mean R > 0 · kept ≥ 3R rate ≥ **3 × this population's
own incumbent rate** (the lane's arm — incumbent stop, no target, trail max(SMA10, SMA20) at s10 on ERA A — measured

NAME by summed R · **era rule:** ERA B is too thin for the full bar and can only confirm direction — a cell counts
only if ERA B's mean R has the same sign as ERA A's, with ERA B's n stated; a cell with zero ERA B fires is not
confirmed · pooled n ≥ 150 fires over ≥ 60 names. The clearing count is judged against 654 before any cell is
named. Declared outcomes: (i) cells clear ERA A and ERA B confirms → named, fork on them; (ii) cells clear ERA A
and none is confirmed → "eras disagree — re-score under today's rules", stop there; (iii) zero cells clear ERA A →
the eras agree on a null, fork on the lane.

## What this does not answer

- **Re-entry.** First attempts only, as on 09-01; a fire that stopped and re-fired is not scored, so every
  number understates what a re-entry policy would add or cost.
- **ERA B is thin and coverage-patched.** 16 campaigns, 37 first fires, 0 with 20 sessions; 10 campaigns have no
  September minute bars in prod, so 23 of the 37 fires come from the lane's own records with a cached day 0. ERA B
  can confirm a direction, never carry a bar.
- **The 620 rung is warm-up-sensitive.** 7 of its 203 May–Aug fires re-timed by 0–4 sessions on a warm-up change;
  a pattern whose fire session moves with the seed carries that much slack in every cell.
- **The live stack as-of the fire date is mostly a retired stack** (eras A–C for every May–Aug fire); the
  today's-rules arms answer the forward question, and their n per exit era is printed beside them.
- **Whether prod's intraday coverage hole is a collector defect** — 10 of 16 ERA B names and 9 late ERA A names
  have no September minute bars; this replay reports the fact, it does not diagnose it.
