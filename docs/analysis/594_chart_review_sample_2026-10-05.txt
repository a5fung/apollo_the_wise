gap_pct, which is the price at scan time (pre-market or 09:50 ET) vs the prior close. On the daily bars CHRN

==== THE RULE, STATED BEFORE THE REBUILD RAN ====

day's volume >= 3x the prior 20-session average. Arm A = HIGH-tier alert rows only, one per (ticker, day) at
the day's best score (the first build also let MODERATE alerts in). Arm B = the day's best score < 50, any
alerted date excluded. Window widened to 2026-06-01 .. 2026-09-05 because 08-23..09-05 alone held almost no
real EP days; every (ticker, date) he already ruled on in tests/fixtures/must_not_trade_charts.py is dropped
(NVTX, MRLN, NVTS, AVGU, BW, AVAH 08-13, NIQ were the top of the raw lists). Probe:
