## Corrections the check applied

- Rule 1: status changed from already_have to partly. Rising volume over the last 3 sessions is not a gate. It is one of four burst items, and any 3 of the 4 pass (parabolic_detector.py:394-399, :421; parabolic_short.md:54). So a climax can be flagged with volume falling into it. The real hard gates are price acceleration (:303-317) and the climax day being the 20-session volume high (:380-389, :423).
- Rule 2: the down-day count is not stored on every scan row. Rows that fail any of gates 1-4 return before it is computed (parabolic_detector.py:272-317 vs :326-331), so it is empty on those rows. Every climax row passed all the gates, so the replay data claim still stands. The constant at :69 is defined but never used.
- Rule 3: status changed from gap to contradicts_ours. Our Perplexity filter keeps names where no news is found, and a failed call also keeps the name (parabolic_detector.py:514-518, :524-530). It removes any 'other one-shot news event' (:467-469, :473), on the stated view that a spike driven by one piece of news will not reverse (:45-53). That is the reverse of his rule on two of its three branches: the no-news name he refuses can arm, and the fluff-news name he wants can be removed.
- Rule 3 data: a 5-year replay has to recompute the base date (parabolic_detector.py:140-157, :275-276). The stored base_date (db.py:2454) exists only for scans since 05-01.
- Rule 4: 'live income data is not wired (collector.py:153-156)' is wrong. Those lines say only that the FMP income adapter does not feed the EP grade. Live quarterly EPS, revenue and gross margin come from yfinance (fundamentals.py:136-161, :229-299); net income is read only as a fallback for deriving EPS (:244-254). EPS and sales growth are stored, but kept only 30 days (db.py:705-715, :11937). None of this is read on the parabolic path.
