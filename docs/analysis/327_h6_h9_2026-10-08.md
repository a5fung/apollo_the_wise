# #327 delayed-entry lane — H6, H7, H8, H9, 2026-10-08

The last four of the six untested hypotheses in `327_hypotheses_2026-09-27.md` (H10/H11 ran 10-06:
`327_h10_h11_2026-10-06.md`). $0 replays on captured + pre-pulled data, each run by one Opus agent and re-derived
from the raw files by a second (workflow wf_0713bf71-888; outputs `scripts/probes/_327_real_ep/h6_*`…`h9_*`).
MEASUREMENT ONLY — stops, exits, sizing and the live same-day re-entry ban (R3) are his.

## Method and population

- **Campaigns:** the 277 live EP alerts 2026-05-01..09-11 — ERA A = alerts before 08-22 (261 campaigns, 595 first
  fires), ERA B = from 08-22 (16 campaigns, 37 first fires); never pooled.
- **Data:** `daily.tsv` + `sat_daily_long.tsv` (prod `mi_daily_closes` 2024-01-02..10-05; identical on all 51,274
  overlapping bars; AKTS/LIFE/FIG cut at their multi-month gaps — earlier rows are a different security),
  `minute.tsv.gz` + `sat_day0_minutes.tsv.gz` (EP-day 1-min bars; H7 only).
- **Outcomes:** the 09-27 program's walkers (anchors reproduced exactly before any result: e.g. H6's walker
  matches 5,056 of 5,056 re-entry rows and the rerun's winner columns), the TAIL (≥ 3R share) beside the mean,
  drop-best-two, each against the hypothesis's VERBATIM pass bar.

## Results

| | Verdict (verified) | The reading |
|---|---|---|
| **H6** lane geometry (20-session cap, first fire, EP-day anchors) | **FAIL** | The lane SEES the big moves: of 47 launches (+50% from a post-EP low within 40 sessions, n = 217 campaigns) 45 start inside its windows (bar needed ≥ half outside). [Verifier: counting only the lane's first fire per pattern, 17 of 47 (36%) started with no attempt open — still far from the bar.] The hand-off exit keeps 7 of 11 winner fires at +3.8R (bar 8 at +5.5R); every slow exit (21-day, 50-day, 65-EMA) is WORSE at session 60 than at 20. ERA B unreadable at 40+ sessions until 10-23. |
| **H7** day-0 afternoon re-entry (his TEAM shape) | **NOT TESTABLE** | Fires on only 51 of 260 readable ERA A EP days (bar n ≥ 100); those 51 show no edge vs same-day controls (−1.3 to +2.0 points depending on the yardstick; ±6 noise). [Verifier: the ADR yardstick used the EP-day close, unknown at a day-0 fill — direction unreliable; TEVA's 0.2%-wide stop, not TEAM, drives the stop-arm mean.] Scope would reverse R3 — his call regardless. |
| **H8** EP-high reclaim after a dip | **FAIL** | No better than a random session on the same campaigns: −3.3 points on 131 fires (variant), −2.9 on 28 strict fires (bar +5 on ≥ 100). |
| **H9** stops/targets on the supply ladder | **FAIL** | A stop under the nearest cleared level keeps only 1 of the 11 big-winner entries at ≥ 3R (the lane's stop keeps 4, a one-ADR stop 7; bar 9), at every buffer, with or without the target. In capped dollars the ladder loses MORE than the lane's stop once the fires it cannot take count as 0 (−23.3 vs −15.4 dollar-R on 595 ERA A fires). |

**Across all six (H6–H11):** none passes its bar. The consistent reading: the lane is in position on the moves
that matter (H6); what loses money is the stop and exit — and in real capped dollars (H10) the wide-stop advantage
vanishes. No entry variant tested (H7, H8, H11) or ladder placement (H9) beats what the lane already does.
