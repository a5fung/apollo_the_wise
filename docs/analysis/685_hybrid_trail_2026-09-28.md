## Method and population

  broker's GTC stop at that price rounded to cents (raise-only), and sells at the close if the close is

## What this does not answer

- **Out-of-sample:** the held-out block has no line test, so the sign is confirmed on nothing. The
  discovery block is the 09-06 population that already chose the intraday trail.
- **The population was widened after the first run** (to include live-admitted names the harness cannot
  re-score, VICR among them). Both rules are reported and agree on the order of the arms; the widened
  rule shrinks every gap (close-only 4.7R → 4.1R worse) without changing the answer. A reader who wants
  the pre-registered read alone has it in `study_out_prereg.txt`.
- **One hybrid, not the best hybrid:** k ∈ {2, 3}, the bottom third, 10:30 and 1.0 × ADR were fixed, not
  swept; a different signature (volume, bar count, undercut depth) is untested. A3 changes two things at
  once (the grace and the wider catastrophic stop), so its −0.7R cannot be attributed to either alone.
- **Execution:** every close sale books at the 16:00 close with no slippage; live sells at 16:45 with an
  after-hours market order, and the 09-11 rejection shows that path can fail outright. Signature sales book
  at the next minute's open. Live arms breakeven and the +8R partial on a 5-minute poll; the walker does it
  on the minute bar.
- **The minute-bar hole is not random:** the 5 unreadable names were never live positions and 4 of them
  favour the hold arms; the 5-arm pairing understates the hold arms by roughly the THC / HTFL / ARGX gains
  (about +1.5R across the three), which would not change the order of the arms.
- **Twenty trades carry every difference**, and two of them (FTK, INFQ) carry most of the cost. Drop-the-
  best-two totals are shown so the reader can see how much rides on them; the order of the arms survives it.
- **VICR and INTC are open** in every arm; whatever they do next is not in any total.

## THE LINE

- Nothing here is a change. Any change to the live trail — close-based, signature, grace, or a different
  catastrophic stop — is CHANGE_PROCESS, needs his sign-off, and would have to beat the touch stop on a
  population that does not yet exist (an out-of-sample block with line tests).
- **A catastrophic stop must always rest at the broker** (#646 / #649). Every arm above keeps one; the
  arm that keeps the least (close-only, floor at the hard stop or breakeven) produced no loss beyond
  −1.5 money-R here — but "no blow-up on 79 trades" is not the same as "cannot blow up".
- **A fact for his attention, outside this study's question:** the live close-below sale of OKTA on 09-11
  was rejected by the broker because the resting stop held the shares. Whether the exit is now retried
  after cancelling the stop is #646's verify, not this study's.
