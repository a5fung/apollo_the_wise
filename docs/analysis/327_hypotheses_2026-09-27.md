## 1. The answer first

- **What is established:** the lane finds the turns (it fired on all 7 big winners) but loses at the stop. The stop sits a few tenths of a normal day's range under the entry — inside the day's own noise — so two trades in three are stopped, and it kept only 4 of the 11 big-winner entries where a one-day-range stop keeps 7–9 (`327_real_ep_rerun` §D, n = 11 winner fires / ~616 other fires). **What is NOT established is the fix:** the wider stop paid in May and lost on the current rules' EPs (10 of 13 lose, `327_reentry_stop_study`).
- **Hypothesis 1 — the stop is placed at the wrong distance AND under the wrong low.** Every lane stop is a low printed minutes before the entry that has never been tested; his TEAM stop sat under a low that had held two hours. Test (all $0, data on disk): which of three stop rules — a width floor, a stop scaled to the stock's range AFTER the gap, or "only enter when the stop-low is old / tested" — keeps ≥ 7 of the 11 winner entries at a lower cost on the rest, with the same sign in both eras.
- **Hypothesis 2 — the trigger buys the first five-minute close back over the line, not a stock that has stopped falling.** A third of close-reclaim fires and a quarter of low-reclaim fires are on the opening bar; five in six reclaims undercut and reclaim on the same day; no pattern reads volume. His rule is approach → slow down → stop falling → base → turn. ⚠ The evidence cuts both ways (later fires are no better; all 11 winner entries came in the first two sessions), so this is a test, not a conclusion.
- **Hypothesis 3 — the eras disagree because the tape or the kind of stock changed, not because of the 08-22 rule change.** The slide began in the alerts BEFORE the 08-22 rule change (May +0.11R → Aug −0.31R per fire on the lane's arm), every big winner was May–July, ERA B admits $115 stocks where ERA A admitted $27, and fires in a choppy tape almost never reach 3R. If so, waiting for November cannot settle it — the tests are on data we already hold.
- **One check before anything builds on it:** the single positive on real EPs ("the reclaim patterns pick a better-than-random session, +6 points") may be an artefact of fires bunching on day 2, where the day's range is 1.4× the yardstick the control uses. $0 to re-check. Do it first.
- **What the lane should be watching for from today:** (a) whether a winner's first fire survives under the wider stops the lane already records beside its own (three-quarters and one full day's range under the entry) and not the tight one; (b) whether the fires that survive two sessions are the later-in-the-day / higher-volume ones; (c) the market regime on the fire date. **Two fixes found here were made the same day** (see the VERIFIED section): the 09-29 accrual gate no longer counts losers first, and TEAM's low-of-day time is corrected.
- **Selection is not dead — it was scored against the wrong target.** The one selection test judged each feature by whether a fire made 4R under the tight stop that throws most winners away; the EP-day features that survived on day 1 (extension, tightness, catalyst expectedness) were never joined to this lane at all. That is a $0 re-cut.

## 7. The mid-November re-read

**The rate, verified today:** 25 live EP alerts 08-27 → 09-25 (prod `mi_ep_alerts`, source = live), i.e. ~25
campaigns a month. By 11-15 roughly **49 ERA B campaigns** will have 15 sessions; at ERA A's big-winner rate
(7 of 270 = 2.6%, rerun §D) that is **~1.3 expected big winners with a ~28% chance of none**. Low-reclaim first
fires run ~0.8 per campaign (rerun: 13 on 16), so **~40 low-reclaim fires** — a 95% band of about ±14 points on
the +2 ADR-before-−1 ADR rate, against ERA A's +6.1-point edge. (The rerun's "~60 campaigns / ~150 fires" used a
faster August rate; the verified rate is the one above.) **A keep-or-kill call on mean R in November would rest on
0–3 names.** So the re-read is pre-registered as a tail count and a set of direction-only checks, written now.

**Pre-registered, in this order:**

1. **Unit = campaign**, not fire. Population = every live EP alert with alert_date ≥ 08-22 and ≥ 15 sessions by
   11-15, one per (ticker, alert_date). If the EP rules change before then, alerts after that date are **ERA C**
   and are reported separately, never pooled.
2. **Primary — the tail count per recorded stop:** for the incumbent, `_025`, `_075`, `_100` on first attempts:
   winner fires (the rerun's +50%/+30%-held label AND the slow-leader label +25% at s40 where 40 sessions exist)
   kept ≥ 3R, against mean R on the non-winner fires. Prior = ERA A's 4 of 11 (incumbent) vs 7–9 of 11 (1× ADR) at
   −0.32R vs −0.14…−0.19R. **Readable when:** ≥ 3 winner campaigns exist in ERA B. Below that, report the count and
   stop.
3. **Direction-only (sign, with n):** (a) the H2-corrected entry edge per pattern; (b) fire-session stop share under
   the incumbent vs `_075`; (c) share of first attempts stopped within 2 sessions that close above the entry 10
   sessions later (shake-out share; ERA A prior 24–46% by month, lens); (d) the lane's arm by regime on the fire
   date. **Readable as direction when** n ≥ 30 fires per cell; a sign that agrees with ERA A on all four is
   "consistent", disagreement on any is "eras still disagree" — neither is a verdict.
4. **Minimum detectable effect, stated:** a mean-R difference of +0.075R/fire (the 1× ADR low-reclaim trail cell ex-May,
   SD 1.46R, n = 142, lens) needs ~1,500 fires at 2 SE. **No mean-R bar is set for forward n under ~1,000.**
5. **What makes November readable earlier:** fork (a) of the rerun (re-score May–Aug under today's rules) lets the
   ERA A campaigns today's rules would still admit be read WITH ERA B — the only way to borrow n. Its deterministic
   part is $0; its catalyst part is paid and his authorisation.
6. **Prerequisites that must be true by then or the read is blind:** G1 fixed (the gate does not fire on losers-only),
   G3 (forward bars persisted for the lane's campaigns), G5 (regime + alert id stamped, or joined at read time).
