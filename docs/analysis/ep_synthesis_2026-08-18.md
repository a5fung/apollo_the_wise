## §3 — THE GATE PATTERN: what n=3 does and does not support

ALOY died on a 60-day cooldown (9.8×ADR) · BCAR on the $500M mcap floor (14.7× at $227M) · AMRC on
the 09:45 ORB cutoff (8.8×, crossed 09:49). Not one died on the gap floor being argued about.

- **What it does NOT support (measured):** softening the thresholds. Every relaxation cell was
  priced: the near-floor band (gap ≥8%) holds 0 winners in every column; the gate-clean relaxed
  pool's tail rate (1.6–1.9%) is no better than the book it would join (2.0%); ~84 extra
  candidates/month competing for 5 slots is negative in the return-on-capital frame.
- **What is honestly coincidence-grade:** "three DIFFERENT gates." With a stack of ~10 gates and 3
  (censored) winners, three different killers is roughly what chance predicts. Do not build a
  theory of gates on it.
- **What it DOES support (structural, and consistent with ARGX and EROC beyond the n=3):** no
  single-gate tune can recover the class — the false-negative rate is spread thinly across the
  whole stack. The shared feature of every winner-killing rejection, in and out of this study, is
  that it was **one-shot, irrevocable, and left no trace**: the cooldown never looks at the name in
  front of it, the mcap floor never sees the setup, the 09:45 cutoff never revisits, the top-20 cap
  (EROC, 7.89× and still running) never re-ranks. **That is the cross-cutting half of the
  operator's zones-with-triggers instinct, and it is the half the evidence supports:** not bands
  instead of lines, but *a second look instead of oblivion*. His own architecture already contains
  the pattern — proximity-not-touch is exactly "the boundary is not a cliff" — and the delayed-entry
  family is exactly the recovery lane, currently pointed at the wrong population (all declined
  names, rather than *exceptional* rejected names).

**Verdict, stated plainly: the zones instinct is half-confirmed.** The soften-the-floor half is
unsupported and measurably dilutive; the give-rejection-a-recovery-path half is supported by every
winner loss we can name, and no study or mechanism currently addresses it.

## §4 — WHAT IS BEING SYSTEMATICALLY MISSED

Not "next tests" — classes of thing nobody has looked at, given everything above.

1. **The R→dollar translation under the binding size cap.** The target ("winners that pay for the
   whole system") is a DOLLAR statement; every program number is in R; and the position-size cap
   binds on 33 of 95 rows in the bracket read — meaning planned risk ≠ deployed risk on a third of
   the book, so R totals do not translate to dollars uniformly. If the cap binds hardest exactly on
   the volatile, expensive names where the tail lives, the dollar tail is thinner than the R tail
   and the sufficiency arithmetic quietly overstates. Unmeasured; checkable in an afternoon at $0
   from rows we already hold. MANE already showed the inverse distortion ($3,900 of stock behind
   $24 of nominal risk). **This is the one place the whole target arithmetic could be wrong without
   any finding above being wrong.**
2. **Earnings inside the hold window.** Median ≥10R winner peaks at day 46; era C is designed to
   hold for weeks; every such hold traverses the next print. Named once in §0b's "not considered"
   list, owned by nobody, and it interacts with era C directly: the first era-C runner will face an
   earnings date mid-hold and there is no stated policy, measurement, or even flag for it.
3. **The rejection-recovery surface** (from §3). Every gate's NO is final and unlogged from the
   name's perspective. A measure-only "rejected-but-exceptional" watch trail — names any gate
   rejects while (say) the rank shadow scores them top-decile — would have held ALOY, BCAR, AMRC,
   ARGX and EROC, costs nothing live, and generates exactly the evidence the operator's
   zones-with-triggers ruling would need. Whether anything ever ACTS on it is his fork; *observing*
   it is not.
4. **The conversion rehearsal.** The sharpest question the target hangs on — *"when next month's
   tail winner alerts, does TODAY'S system convert it?"* — has never been asked directly. The
   bounded-rule sim covered 43 trades TAKEN; nobody has walked the 15 SURFACED tail winners through
   today's full stack (today's gates, era-C bracket, current exits) and reported where each dies
   now vs where it died then. $0, deterministic, and it converts "we changed a lot in August" into
   a measured statement about whether the leak that lost INTC and SMCI is actually closed.
5. **Winner-N.** Every winner-side read now stalls at n=3 and stays anecdote-grade until ~09-15.
   The three-arm winner reference set (path step 5) is the only way to grow winner statistics
   without waiting, is fully specified, and keeps being displaced by path studies. It is queued
   rather than missed — but the displacement pattern is itself the "dozen paths" failure mode.
