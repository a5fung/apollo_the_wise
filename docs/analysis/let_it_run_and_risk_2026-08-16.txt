  WHERE DAILY RESOLUTION UNDERSTATES THE RISK (all conservative floors):
    1. Days 1+ MAE uses DAILY LOWS — the true minute-path excursion is >= the daily low
       only in timing, never magnitude, BUT sub-day sequencing is invisible: a day that
       dipped -3R then closed green counts once; multiple intraday round-trips don't.
       Magnitude itself IS the daily low, so the floor is exact per-day; what is
       understated is DURATION and path (margin calls, panic behaviour, intraday
       decision pressure are unmodelled).
    2. No minute bars exist for ANY of the 43 hold windows beyond day 0 (April-May
       cohort; alert-day minute capture began 2026-07-28 and the 08-15 backfill covers
       alert ticker-days, not 60-day hold windows). Day-0 excursions ARE minute-true.
    3. The close-basis exits assume the day's official CLOSE is attainable (MOC-style).
       Auction slippage on thin names is unmodelled, as everywhere in this work.
    4. Same-day stop-vs-target ordering at daily resolution is unknowable; the sim
       assumes stop-first (conservative for the intraday arms; the close-basis arm is
       unaffected except via the partial's bank).
    5. One regime (Apr-Aug 2026), reconstructed not lived, no slippage, and the 43-name
       60d cohort is ALL early-period — no out-of-sample half exists until October.
