## R1 — The correlated book: one theme in five costumes  ⚠ TOP RISK

**Scenario.** A hot-theme week (quantum, AI-silicon — exactly the families the theme engine
currently over-produces). Five EP HIGHs from one family fire across 2-3 sessions; all five pass
every gate (they are five "independent" tickers); the book is now ONE bet at ~100% deployment
(5 × 20% position cap). Sector-wide overnight reversal gaps all five through their stops. Stops
floor the damage but don't prevent it: −10-20% account in one open.

**Anchored exposure.** No theme/sector/correlation gate exists anywhere in the entry path
(`_check_safeguards` full gate list, `live_tracker.py:99-244`, `entry_pipeline.py:350-362` —
verified absence); ADR 0020 (multi-setup book) covers per-setup regime multipliers, NOT
cross-slot correlation (0020:89-98); no cross-setup same-ticker dedup beyond same-day
(`entry_pipeline.py:307-330`). The premarket gap alert (7/8, `premarket_gap_risk_scan`, 9:00 ET)
is **telemetry-only and fires after the gap**. Stops are floors, not prevention
(`order_manager.py:214-222`; halt/LULD = reactive).

**Aggravator (the system steers INTO this):** the theme axis (ADR 0015) credits Accelerating-
theme membership toward HIGH — selection is *biased toward* correlated entries by design. The
concentration risk is not incidental; it is the strategy's own preference unpriced at the
portfolio level.

**Blast × probability:** −10-20% · moderate-and-rising (theme-heavy tape + 5 slots + no gate).
**Mitigation design (two stages, filed #452):**
1. **Telemetry NOW (no-money, ship fast):** EOD digest line + audit row — current book's
   theme/sector overlap + aggregate overnight notional ("3/4 open positions share family X;
   overnight exposure 61% of equity"). Visibility before authority, per house discipline.
2. **The gate (CHANGE_PROCESS + sign-off):** a family-slot cap at entry — max N (rec: 2) open
   positions per theme-family (family = the ADR 0025 Stage-A family notion — one mechanism,
   reused); skip_reason `block:theme_concentration`. Backtest: replay historical books (paper +
   live history) to measure how often the cap would have bound + what it would have cost/saved.
   Operator fork: cap value + whether HIGH-conviction (game_changer) earns an exemption.

## R3 — Calibration blindness: the monitor sleeps through the first 20 trades

**Scenario.** Every performance expectation rests on one 12-month, mixed-regime window (#268b:
n=399, 2025-06-09→2026-05-04, +0.95R/30% — no regime stratification). The tape is now Choppy/
Correcting. If live expectancy is actually negative in this regime, the kill/scale bands — the
ONLY mechanical health monitor — say **nothing until 20 closed trades** (`kill_scale_bands.py`
sample floor). At ~1.5 closed/week that is ~3 months of silence; 20 × ~1% risk ≈ −15-20% is
reachable before the monitor's first word. The drawdown breaker floors this at ~−12% (BLOCK
tier) — the real backstop — but it was field-tested on **paper** (5/22 trip) and never live.

**Anchored exposure.** Bands: `kill_scale_bands.py:29-53` (one band set, no regime dimension);
ep_threshold + composite_min=22 are rule-based, never data-calibrated (`regime.py:185-199`,
`constants.py:312-330`); no drift detector on strategy performance below band thresholds
(verified absence — the L2/L3 anomaly system watches ops metrics, not expectancy).

**Blast × probability:** −12% (breaker-floored) · elevated in the current regime.
**Mitigation (filed #454):** (1) **early-window drift line** — from closed trade 5 onward, the
weekly review prints rolling live expectancy vs the calibration envelope percentile
(informational, no authority — the n<20 caveat printed with it); (2) **regime-stratified
envelope re-run** — one analysis card: re-cut the #268b cohort by regime label, publish
per-regime envelopes so the bands' one-size assumption is either validated or replaced at the
next quarterly band review; (3) **verify the drawdown breaker's live wiring end-to-end** (a #151
paper exercise on the live-mode code path — the backstop everything above leans on has never
fired live).

## R4 — Intraday portfolio blindness: six naked hours

**Scenario.** Five positions open by 9:45. A midday market break (−7% on the book by noon).
The drawdown breaker evaluates at **16:12 ET snapshots only**; the daily-loss gate counts
**realized** losses and is checked **only at the next entry attempt**. Between 9:45 and 16:12
nothing portfolio-level watches the book — per-position stops are the only actors, each blind
to the others. New entries at 11:00 still size at full multiplier while the book bleeds.

**Anchored exposure.** `drawdown_breaker.py:12-16` (16:12 cadence), `live_tracker.py:161-193`
(realized-only, per-entry evaluation). **Blast × probability:** compounds R1/R3 rather than
standalone · moderate. **Mitigation (filed #455):** an **intraday portfolio-drawdown check on
the existing 15-min reconcile cycle** (it already reads positions + equity): compute live
drawdown from the 30d peak; on crossing WATCH/REDUCE intraday → Telegram + audit (alert-first);
optionally (operator fork, stage 2) apply the REDUCE sizing tier to *new* entries same-day.
Reuses the breaker's existing tier logic — no new thresholds invented; CHANGE_PROCESS for the
stage-2 authority step.

## The 20%-loss decomposition (the answer to the title question)

−10-15% in one gap) landing inside R3 (a regime the calibration never priced, while the monitor
is below its sample floor) with R4 ensuring no intraday brake slows the bleed, floored only by
the (live-untested) drawdown breaker at −12% — which R1 can jump straight past in a single
overnight gap. **The single highest-leverage fix is R1's family-slot cap** (it breaks the
compound chain at its widest link), followed by R3's breaker-verification (the backstop
everything else leans on must be proven live).
