## 1. R-attribution: selection creates it · entry preserves it · management leaks it

**SELECTION creates the edge.** The deterministic floor alone replays at **+0.90R/trade, 28% win
(n=410, 12mo)**. The judge's headline add is noise (+0.05R), but its **promote arm is real:
+1.26R × n=83/yr** of judge-promoted MODERATEs — the single measured selection improvement. Both
context axes point the same way (theme Accelerating +18.0%/77% vs +9.6%/56%; structure
Stage-2+tight 64% vs 56% — calibration-grade N, directional). Judge grading itself validated
against the operator (5/5 demotes justified; where judge and mechanical composite disagree, the
judge's side was right 11/12 times forward). The filter suite is roughly calibrated (revenue pin
correct by backward-check; M&A filter needs the #416 FP amendment; nothing else mispriced).

**ENTRY preserves it — and is DONE.** Every tested variation on the entry makes it worse,
monotonically: 5-min OR −0.80R vs baseline (dead) · skip-wide-open refuted (coverage artifact;
91% of its "edge" was 5 outliers) · every wider stop degrades +1.40R → +0.14R in order ·
window extension R-negative wide, no robust edge near (settled 7/9-7/10). The entry-side leak
that remains (day-0 stopouts ≈ −$6.1k on the paper cohort; the thin gap-through class → ADR
0029 C1) is **half the size of the management leak**. The w2 program's own verdict stands:
stop tuning the bracket.

**MANAGEMENT leaks it.** MFE capture on partial-taken winners: **18%** ($5.9k kept of $32.8k
peak, N=10) — the round-trippers (SMCI +11.7% peak → closed a LOSS; PURR 0%; CRSR 15%) hand back
what selection bought. The #306 sweep isolated the mechanism and the fix: the **peak-lock floor
alone is worth +$8,075 on an 11-winner cohort (capture 23%→52%), plateau-robust, losers and
runners untouched** — the largest quantified $ lever in the entire program, and its forward
shadow is ALREADY DEPLOYED (F1, 7/9; review gated ≥10 rows / 8/06).

## 2. The unifying fact: the edge IS the tail

Every cohort in the corpus is fat-tail-carried: medians ≈ −1R everywhere; top-5 = 91% of
skip-wide-open's R; top-3 = 190% of Day-2-ORB's total; drop the top-5 winners from ANY stop
arm and expectancy ≈ 0. This is the Qullamaggie/Pradeep shape working as designed — and it has
three hard consequences that should govern v1.1:
1. **Anything that clips the tail is poison** at any win-rate gain (why the R1-R4 selectivity
   bundle, +15.1pp win at −32.7% volume, must NOT ship unbundled — the cut candidates include
   tail producers; 69% of dropped Day-1 names went on to +5%).
2. **Anything that keeps the tail while cutting giveback compounds** (the peak-lock passed
   exactly this test: round-trippers locked, CRSR-class runners not clipped).
3. **One correlated wipeout erases a year of +0.95R** (the premortem's R1: the book can
   concentrate into one theme while every per-trade number stays green — tail-shaped edges die
   by portfolio events, not by trade-level decay).
