## The decision this serves

The acting floor (read from `LIVE_RULES.txt` 2026-08-31, generated from code:
`ep_detector.py:180`) is **prior close ≥ $5.00 AND prior-day volume ≥ 50,000 shares** — a name
failing either is never a candidate, at any gap size. It is our largest single exclusion class.
The decision: is this floor costing us real, TRADEABLE EPs (P1/P14), and if so what level or
shape would the operator consider instead? **This is a DETECTION CRITERION — any change is the
operator's sole authority (THE LINE). This document is evidence only.**

What would change the decision: if the names below the floor that genuinely gapped at the open
were mostly untradeable at our size, the floor is right and the weekly review's headline is
noise. They are not (49 of 89 are cleanly tradeable, below), so the question becomes level,
shape, and cost.

## Method / population

- **Rows:** `mi_ep_missed_outcomes`, `skip_category = 'd1_universe_floor'` — **941 rows,
  671 distinct tickers**. ⚠ This table was 60% corrupt until 2026-08-29 (#595): most rows'
  scanned gap was a pre-market print that faded before the bell. **Every setup-count here
  filters `setup_at_open IS TRUE`** (open ≥ 9% over prior close, the acting gap floor) —
  **89 rows, 79 distinct tickers**, exactly reproducing the numbers in the card.
- **⚠ WINDOW CORRECTION — this is 5 trading days, not 30.** The category's rows span
  **2026-08-24 → 2026-08-28 only** (the category was mis-bucketed into `filter_other` until the
  08-25 SQL-side fix; 08-24 was reconciled). Every "per 30 days" framing of this cohort —
  including this card's own brief and the weekly review's "941 in 7 days" — is really **one
  week of one tape** (live account in drawdown WATCH per LIVE_RULES; regime row not captured).
  All per-day rates below divide by 5.
- **Joins:** D-1 and D0 price/volume from `mi_daily_closes` (full Polygon grouped-daily
  universe; 0 of 89 setups missing either join). 20-day ADV$ and 5-day run-up computed the
  same way.
- **Outcome column:** `max_high_5d` is **maximum favourable excursion from the D0 open, as a
  RATIO** (verified: table max 8.22 = +822%; the cohort's max is DAIC 5.19 = +519%). It is
  positive on nearly every row by construction and is **never a bankable return**. `ret_5d` is
  NULL on **all 89** setups — the forward window hasn't completed — so MFE is the only outcome
  available, and it is **truncated** for late-week rows (08-28's window is ~1 session).
- **Era:** all five days post-date the current admission rules (9% gap floor, RT gap authority,
  lattice, separation score, pre-score shortlist). One era, one regime.

## The numbers

### 1. Tradeability — the crux (question 1)

Actual live size, read from prod: equity ≈ **$4,900** (`mi_account_equity_snapshots` 08-28),
recent live fills **$300–$1,000 notional, ~$50 risk** (`mi_live_trades`, n=15 shown in capture).
Test: our notional ≤ 1% of the name's **EP-day** traded dollar volume (≥ $100k for $1k), and
open ≥ $1 as a spread proxy (we hold no quote history for these names — see limits).

| cohort (of the 89 real setups) | n | MFE mean | MFE med | ≥30% MFE |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| tradeable at today's $1k size | 58 | +34% | +13% | 14 |
| tradeable at design $20k size (sim account) | 48 | +37% | +14% | 13 |
| …and first-appearance (60d cooldown) and passes the 50% extension cap | **49** | +34% | +13% | 11 |

**≈ 10 tradeable, cooldown-fresh, extension-clean real setups per day** are currently excluded
by the floor. Their EP-day dollar volume is large (median $30M; DAIC traded $135M, CELU $454M)
— **D-1 volume barely predicts EP-day volume for this class (log-log r = 0.44, n=89)**: the
floor screens yesterday's liquidity, but fills happen in today's, which the pre-market shares
floor and real-time volume checks already measure.

### 2. The cliff (question 2) — there isn't one on price; the shape matters more

Sweep on the 941 floor rows (admitted = D-1 close ≥ P and D-1 volume ≥ V). Both P14 numbers at
every level; "noise" = rows that never gapped at the open (`setup_at_open` false).

| level | real setups admitted (of 89) | noise rows admitted (of 851) | tradeable@$1k of the setups |
|---|---|---|---|
| **P≥$5 V≥50k (current)** | **0** | 1 | 0 |
| P≥$4 V≥50k | 3 | 36 | 3 |
| P≥$3 V≥50k | 13 | 93 | 13 |

Recall grows smoothly ~2× per dollar down, and the setup:noise ratio stays ≈1:8 the whole way —
**no natural cliff in the price dimension**. The volume dimension is small (only 85 of 941 rows
are volume-floor hits; 7 setups).

### 3. A dollar-volume floor dominates the two-part gate (question 3)

| D-1 $-volume floor | real setups (of 89) | noise rows (of 851) | tradeable@$1k |
|---|---|---|---|
| ≥ $5M | 20 | 54 | 15 |
| ≥ $2M | 29 | 107 | 22 |
| **≥ $1M** | **41** | **177** | **30** |
| ≥ $250k | 57 | 384 | 39 |

At equal recall (~40 setups), **$1M D-1 dollar volume admits 43% fewer noise rows than P≥$1
(177 vs 309)** and beats it on grading load too (next section). A $3 stock trading 5M shares
passes; a $50 stock trading 3k shares fails — the gate finally measures the thing it exists for.

### 4. What it would cost (question 4)

Grading is the next real gate (top-20 pre-score shortlist per tick, score ≥65 bar). A noise row
costs a grading slot only if its scan-tick gap ≥9% — with the RT overlay ON since 08-27, the
scan gap IS the live-price gap. Current graded volume: 1–8 names/day; alerts 1–5/day.

| option | +real setups/day | +noise/day reaching grading | dies later anyway? |
|---|---|---|---|
| current floor | 0 | 0 | — |
| P≥$1 V≥50k | 7.8 | 8.4 | see below |
| **$-vol ≥ $1M** | **8.2** | **5.2** | see below |
| no floor | 17.8 | 24.0 | see below |

Downstream fate of admitted setups (n=89): the extension cap kills only 9, cooldown dedup 10 —
**the mechanical gates do NOT quietly re-kill this cohort**. The fade-class noise IS killed
before money: the at-submission gap recheck (`ep_rt_entry_gap_recheck`, live) rejects a name
whose live gap is back under 9% — its cost is grading slots and alert noise, not dollars. What
we cannot replay at $0 is the catalyst/score bar: how many of the ~8 setups/day would actually
score ≥65 is unknown — the real alert-volume increase is bounded above by ~8/day and is
probably far smaller, since sub-$5 names lose all 15 liquidity points and most microcap gaps
have no gradeable catalyst.

### 5. Adversarial (n=89, 5 days, one tape)

- **Best 3 removed** (DAIC +519%, BRNX +147%, PMI +146% — all 08-24): the 49-name surviving
  tradeable set falls from mean +34% MFE to **mean +18.7%, median +12.3%; 8 of 46 still ≥30%**
  (n=46). The finding survives, at half the headline.
- **Clustering:** setups appear every one of the 5 days (7–14/day) — not one event. But five
  days is one tape; 08-24's monsters may be one hot Monday. The 08-28 day's low mean (+5%) is a
  truncated window, not evidence of decay.
- **How much is fitting?** The $1M level was chosen by eye off this one week's grid; treat the
  LEVEL as unfitted-but-unvalidated. The SHAPE claim (dollar-volume dominates price×volume at
  equal recall) and the MECHANISM claim (D-1 liquidity doesn't predict EP-day fill, r=0.44) are
  structural and should replicate. The category now populates daily at $0 — 20 sessions of
  accrual puts ~350 setups behind the same cut.
- The card's "biggest by an order of magnitude" overstates rows: 941 vs `outside_top20`'s 791
  in the same table. It IS the biggest, and the only one that is pure D-1 data.

## What this does not answer

- **Whether these are OUR kind of EP.** MFE says they move; it cannot say they have the
  catalyst-driven character MAGNA53 trades. Many sub-$2 gappers are pumps/dilution plays the
  judge would (rightly) refuse. Untested at $0 — this is the biggest open question.
- **Spread.** No quote history exists for these names; open ≥ $1 is a proxy, not a measurement.
  At $50 risk per trade, a 3% spread eats material R. A shadow week recording live NBBO on
  floor-skipped gappers would answer it.
- **Bankable return.** All outcomes are MFE; `ret_5d` is NULL on the whole cohort until the
  forward windows complete (~2026-09-05). Nothing here says a trade would have made money —
  that needs the bracket replay on minute bars (the #577 lesson: +15R MFE names still paid −1R).
- **Score/alert volume.** How many admitted names clear the 65 bar — needs grading, not $0.
- **Any other regime.** Five sessions of one tape (drawdown WATCH week); the regime label
  itself was not captured.
