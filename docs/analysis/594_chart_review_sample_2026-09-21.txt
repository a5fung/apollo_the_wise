==== METHOD (so a ruling is not read as more than it is) ====
Window: alert/scan dates 2026-08-09 .. 20 sessions settled (sample #1 ended 2026-08-08).
Outcome: 20-session close vs the day's OPEN, from mi_daily_closes directly — NOT
  mi_ep_missed_outcomes, which holds only alerts we did NOT buy and would hide arm A entirely.
Arm A = names that ALERTED and then fell hardest. Arm B = names whose BEST score all day stayed
  under the 50 skip floor, that then ran, with anything we alerted on EXCLUDED.

 1. MRNA 2026-08-19 appeared in arm B as "we scored it below the bar and it ran +36%". We
