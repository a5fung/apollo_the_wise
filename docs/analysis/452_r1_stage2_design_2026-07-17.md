# #452 R1 Stage 2 — the family-slot cap at entry (DESIGN ONLY)

Premortem R1 (docs/analysis/premortem_450_2026-07-11.md): five same-family EP
HIGHs can fill all five slots — one bet at ~100% deployment — and the theme
axis steers selection toward exactly that. Stage 1 (telemetry) shipped
2026-07-17 (`book_concentration.py`, 16:18 ET job, audit series + flag-only
Telegram). Stage 2 is THE GATE — an entry-discipline change, so:
**CHANGE_PROCESS + operator sign-off + replay backtest before any flip
(THE LINE). Nothing in this doc is built.**

## Mechanism

At the `_check_safeguards` step of `entry_pipeline.submit_trade_entry` (the
same per-mode envelope that owns max-positions / daily-loss / drawdown):

1. Compute the candidate ticker's Stage-A families (same code path as Stage 1:
   active-theme membership → `theme_merge_arm.family_of`).
2. Count OPEN positions (status='filled', remaining_shares>0, same
   account_mode) whose families intersect.
3. If count ≥ `MAX_POSITIONS_PER_FAMILY` → skip with
   `block:theme_concentration` (new constant in `broker/skip_reasons.py`,
   `block:*` = counted as pass-through by preflight, consistent with the
   existing cap vocabulary). Alert routing identical to other `block:*` skips.

Config: `MAX_POSITIONS_PER_FAMILY` (rec **2**) — env-tunable like
`MAX_CONCURRENT_LIVE_POSITIONS`; per-mode isolated (paper ≠ live), NULL/0 =
gate off (ship dark, flip by env + restart or DB toggle — operator fork F-C).

## Replay backtest (the sign-off evidence, run BEFORE the flip)

Replay the full mi_live_trades + mi_paper_trades history (plus
mi_ep_missed_outcomes for the would-have-entered pool): walk entries in time
order, maintain the simulated open book, apply the cap, record every
would-have-blocked entry and its realized forward return.
Metrics: (a) how often the cap binds; (b) P&L of blocked entries (cost of the
gate); (c) worst same-family overnight exposure with vs without the cap (the
tail it exists to cut); (d) sensitivity at cap = 1/2/3.
Accept: the cap meaningfully cuts the worst-case same-family exposure while
blocking few strong winners — the operator rules on the (b)-vs-(c) trade.

## Operator forks

- **F-A cap value**: 2 (rec) vs 3. At 5 slots, cap 2 bounds one family to 40%
  of the book.
- **F-B game_changer exemption**: does a `game_changer` catalyst earn a 3rd
  same-family slot? Rec: NO at v1 (the premortem's point is precisely that
  high-conviction correlated entries are the trap); revisit on replay data.
- **F-C rollout**: dark-ship + DB toggle (the THEME_SUBTHEME_ARM idiom) vs
  env-flag. Rec: DB toggle, fail-closed OFF.
- **F-D family notion v2**: Stage-A stem families (13 stems + sector fallback)
  vs the ADR-0032 ecosystem mapping (20 curated operator-signed buckets,
  `mi_theme_ecosystems`). Ecosystems are broader (E-BANKFIN spans banks +
  fintech + brokers) → stricter gate; stems are replay-exact and match the
  premortem rec. Rec: ship Stage 2 on STEMS (one mechanism with Stage 1 + the
  merge arm), file the ecosystem variant as a calibration follow-up once the
  Stage-1 series shows which notion tracks realized correlation better.
