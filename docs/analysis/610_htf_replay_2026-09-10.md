**2026-09-10 · read-only · $0 · #610 analysis half (the evidence #397's money gate is waiting on) ·

⛔ **THE LINE: this document is EVIDENCE. Nothing was changed — no threshold, no strategy, no live
state.** Every constant was read at its shipped value (`_HTF_MIN_ADR_PCT=0.04`,
`_HTF_MIN_ADV_SHARES=500_000`, `_RUNUP_MIN_RATIO=1.90`, `_RUNUP_LOOKBACK_DAYS=40`) and asserted

## The answer in three lines

- **N is no longer the blocker.** Under today's code the five years hold **253 first breakouts
  (351 settled break-rows), 148 of them takeable and settled, with 25 winners** — #397's N≥10
  settled winners exists today, offline.
- **The shipped bet does not pay.** On those 148: capture 16.9% (95% interval 10.9–22.9%, so the
  pre-declared 25% breakeven is excluded), mean **+0.05R ±0.25** (straddles zero), median −1R. Drop the 8 gap-day entries a stop-limit order could not have
  filled and it is **−0.09R** (capture 12.9%). That is the finding.
- **The sourced management protocol (#396 sibling readout) reads +0.74R mean — carried by five
  trades** (85% of the total R; +0.12R without them; median −0.16R), negative in 2022–23 and
  positive in 2024–26. A tail, not an edge you can size from n=147.

## The question

#397 requires N≥10 settled winners from `mi_htf_breakout_shadow`. Prod holds **16 rows, 9 settled,
1 capture** since 2026-06-29; at that rate the evidence is a year away. This morning's measurement
(`610_htf_adr_floor_exclusions_2026-09-10.md`) showed the ADR floor sorts correctly and withdrew
the case for lowering it. The remaining unblock was a **replay**: the chain prod runs live —
detector → break → order shape → settle — executed offline over `mi_daily_closes` (13,620,198
rows, 2021-09-07 → 2026-09-09, 20,041 tickers) under TODAY's code.

## Method — which functions, which bars, which rows

**The shipped functions are called, not re-implemented.** `compute_flag_metrics`,
`prepare_htf_breakout_order`, `_htf_settle_from_bars` and `_htf_management_replay` are imported
from `agents/market_intelligence/flag_detector.py` at HEAD (includes #592's `_flag_resolved_by`
of 09-04, the median ADV of 07-18, the 262-trading-row history of 09-05). A second copy of the
rules would measure a system we do not run.

| stage | prod | replay | bars read |
|---|---|---|---|
| universe | `get_flag_universe(d)`: CS/ADRC or unknown type · close ≥ $5 · 20-session mean dollar volume ≥ $5M · ≥ 60 sessions in 90 days · **AND (rs_top200 OR rs_1m ≥ 80 OR +25% off the 10-session min close)** · carry-forward paths | the same gates **minus the RS/burst OR-block** (RS scores exist only from 2025-12-26 — Risk 2); carry-forwards not reproduced | ≤ d |
| detector | `compute_flag_metrics(last 262 rows ≤ d)`, state from the ticker's rows in [d−5 calendar days, d) | identical call; state threaded identically (mirrors the three prod state queries) | ≤ d |
| break, next session d+1 | 5-min scan: parent = latest scan's TIGHTENING/COILED/TRIGGERED row with close(d) ≤ base_high; fires when price > base_high AND the **intraday volume-pace projection** ≥ adv_20 (median of the 20 sessions ending at d) | **daily proxy:** high(d+1) > base_high AND **full-day volume(d+1)** ≥ adv_20 | d+1 |
| order shape | `prepare_htf_breakout_order(base_high, base_low, sma_10, sma_20, regime_record=None)` | identical call | — |
| settle (Phase 3) | `_htf_settle_from_bars(bars, entry_idx, entry_price, stop, target_r=3)`: fill at base_high, entry bar included, 12 forward sessions, capture +3R / stop −1R / open = mark-to-window | identical call | d+1 … d+13 |
| management (Phase 4 sibling) | `_htf_management_replay`: scale 40% day 3–5 → breakeven → 10/20 EMA trail, hard stop = the Phase-3 stop | identical call, forward bars capped at 300 sessions | d+2 … exit |

**Which (ticker, day) pairs get the detector.** An actionable stage needs a pivot within the 25
sessions before d with `pivot_high / min(low over the 40 sessions ending at the pivot) ≥ 1.9`,
which implies `max(high, 25 sessions before d) / min(low, 65 sessions before d) ≥ 1.9`. That
weaker condition is the pre-screen (637,741 pairs); the detector then runs on every universe day
from 60 sessions before a ticker's first hit to 5 after its last (409,676 calls), state threaded
continuously. Prod's holiday scans (2026-07-03, 09-07) re-score the last session with no bar of
their own; those 174 rows are counted, not compared.

## Pre-declared: what "poor" means — written before the results were read

For a +3R / −1R bet the breakeven capture rate is 25%. **The replayed expectancy is POOR if, on
the first-break-per-episode, takeable (`would_reject_reason IS NULL`) set:** (1) capture rate
< 25%, **or** (2) mean realised R ≤ 0, **or** (3) fewer than 10% of breakouts ever reach +3R of
favourable excursion. Disclosure: a 600-ticker smoke subset (n=8, capture 25%, mean +0.09R) had
been seen — a coin-flip that said nothing about direction.

**Result against the declaration: criterion (1) fires** (16.9%, interval 10.9–22.9% — 25% is
outside it); (2) does not (+0.05R ±0.25, and −0.09R ±0.24 once unfillable entries are removed —
the interval straddles zero either way); (3) does not (17% reach
+3R). **Verdict: poor / no demonstrated edge for the shipped bet.**

## The four ways this goes wrong — each answered

**1. Lookahead.** Detection at d reads only bars ≤ d (262 rows; the 52-week high and the 200-day
are computed from those). The break at d+1 reads d+1's high — a resting stop-limit at base_high
is touched by it — and d+1's **full-day volume, the one end-of-day quantity** (prod reads an
intraday pace projection; the isolated test above measures the gap: 25 breaks prod fired that
the proxy did not, 42 the proxy fired that prod did not, on 1,215 parents). Settlement reads
d+1 … d+13 and nothing later; the management readout reads forward until its own exit. Bars are
split-adjusted as of the pull, so ratio gates are invariant but the $5 / $5M / 500k-share gates see
post-split values retroactively; 6 overlap parents (CRWD) were visibly re-adjusted since their
scan. The settler's entry-bar inclusion resolved 38 of 148 on the break day under the
target-first bound; the remaining 110 read +0.12R.

**2. Survivorship.** Bars were pulled per DATE from Polygon grouped-daily (ingest filter: ticker
≤ 5 characters, no "."), so delisted names are present: **5,872 of 20,041 tickers have their last
bar before 2026** (397 ended in 2021, 1,245 in 2022, 1,626 in 2023, 1,366 in 2024, 1,238 in 2025).
Survivor bias is absent at the bar level. The residual is `mi_security_types`, a current snapshot
(13,846 rows): 6,195 tickers carry no type; prod admits unknowns too, and 27 of the 148 breaks are
unknown-type — their outcomes (2 / 12 / 13, +0.13R, capture 7%) do not carry the result; without
them it is +0.03R. Direction: if anything the delisted-skewed population is where the winners are
NOT. Prod's RS gate is absent before 2025-12-26 (Risk-2 sensitivity above): broader universe,
same sign.

**3. Era.** Replaying 2021–2026 bars under HEAD is intended: the question is what today's rules
would have found and settled, so that #397 has settled outcomes. **The 2022–2025 breakouts are not
a claim about what the system did at the time** — the detector did not exist before 2026-05, and
its rules changed on 06-27/28, 07-18, 09-04 and 09-05. The 97 stage mismatches inside the overlap
are that era effect made visible: 62 before the ADV change, 33 before #592, 2 after.

**4. A result that cannot be negative is worthless.** The thresholds were declared before the run
and the run failed one of them. The result had every opportunity to be negative and took several:
2023 delivered 0 captures in 20; the wick subset reads −0.62R; the fillable set reads −0.09R; the
would-reject class reads −0.18R. No constant was moved (asserted at run start) and no variant was
run. The one structural optimism is the shipped settler's unconditional fill at base_high, which
this document reports rather than fixes: 7 of the 25 winners opened above the order's limit and
never traded back to it.

## What this does NOT answer

- **Intraday behaviour.** A 5-minute tick scan is not a daily bar; the proxy's bias is bounded
  above, not eliminated.
- **The universe before 2025-12-26.** Prod's RS gate cannot be applied there; the sensitivity says
  the sign holds, not that the counts would.
- **Whether a fill-aware settler changes the money answer.** It does — from +0.05R to −0.09R —
  which is why the shipped settler's fill assumption is a finding here, not a footnote.
- **The management protocol's size.** +0.74R rests on five trades; n=147 cannot size a tail.
- **Anything about a different threshold, stop rule, volume gate or parent stage.** The splits are
  recorded with their n so nobody re-derives them; each is a criteria change.
