## What this page adds that we do not currently have

**1. THE 23-EMA IS THE SPINE OF THE WHOLE TRADE.** It appears in four separate annotations — every
add is stopped at it, the trend is defined by "clear respect" for it, and the run ends when price
closes below it on volume. **We do not compute a 23-EMA anywhere.** Our stop is `entry − 2R` off a
one-minute range; this book trails a moving average for the life of the move.

**2. A NAMED VOCABULARY FOR THE BASE.** Pocket pivots ("5-day PP", "PP volume signature"), prior
resistance becoming support, "tight, sideways on light volume" as the constructive state. **This is
exactly the taxonomy he asked for** — *"look at what bases look like"* — and it is written down
rather than needing to be derived.

**3. RELATIVE STRENGTH LEADING PRICE (RSNHBP).** The RS line makes new highs *before* price, and
*throughout the base*. We compute RS percentile ranks; we do not check whether RS is making new
highs ahead of price, which is the actual tell here.

**4. RELATIVE BEHAVIOUR IN A CORRECTION AS A QUALITY SCREEN.** −25.61% against a −35.41% market.
A drawdown-vs-index ratio during a correction — cheap to compute, and we do not.

**5. VOLUME ON NEW HIGHS AS A NEGATIVE.** *"Volume was below average as AMZN made new highs, which
is a sign of weakness."* We treat volume as a positive-only axis; here its ABSENCE at a new high is
the warning.

**6. THE EXIT IS A SEQUENCE, NOT A LEVEL.** Extended above the 10-DMA → sharp big-volume negative
reversal → take partials. Then closing below the 23-EMA/50-DMA → sell some or all. Then volume
expansion on the first close below the 23-EMA since breakout → the run is over. **Three graded
signals, none of which is a fixed R multiple.**

⚠ **This is ONE page and one name, and a winner chosen for a model book — survivorship by
construction.** It is a source of HYPOTHESES to declare and then test, never evidence in itself.
