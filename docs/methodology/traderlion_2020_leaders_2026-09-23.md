# TraderLion 2020 leaders — 13 marked-up charts the operator shared (2026-09-23)

**Source.** The operator photographed 13 annotated pages from a TraderLion book of 2020 market
leaders (StockCharts daily charts, Nov 2019 → Dec 2020, with MA 10/21, EMA 23, MA 50, EMA 65,
MA 200, an RS line against the S&P 500 in the top panel, volume, and four quarters of EPS and
revenue growth). He shared them in chat as *"the marked up charts"* he owed, before deciding on the
chart-vision spend (#519). Images, recompressed to 1800 px and still legible:
`docs/methodology/operator_shared_charts/2026-09-23_traderlion_2020_leaders/` (01_CRWD … 13_TSLA).

**What they are, stated so they are not misused.** Teaching examples, every one of them a WINNER,
annotated after the fact. They show where a disciplined trader buys, adds and sells on a leader;
they say nothing about how often the same patterns fail on non-leaders (survivorship). They are
2020 charts, and `mi_daily_closes` starts 2025-08-18, so none of them can be replayed against our
bars today.

## What the 13 add up to (synthesis — mine, grouped; his source words above are the authority)

| theme | what the charts say | where it touches our build |
|---|---|---|
| **Entries** | a breakout through a pivot from a PROPER base on big volume; a pocket pivot / 5-day pocket pivot; a constructive shakeout to the MA the stock respects, or to a prior-pivot + MA confluence; a reclaim of a key MA on big volume; a break of the declining-tops line | Saturday's Block 5 (entries undetermined for new setups); #354's consolidation family (Confirm / U&R entries) |
| **Which MA** | stock-specific: DOCU 10/21-23 (and *"rarely respects its 50/65"*), CRWD and ZM 50/65, NIO and PTON the 23-EMA; buy and add against the MA the stock has shown it respects | `walk_arm`'s `character_ma` trail already models a per-stock home MA (#631) |
| **Adds** | on constructive pullbacks to the respected MA; SMALLER late in the move; *"no add here, because volume is too light"* | sizing — THE LINE, his call |
| **Sells** | close below the respected MA: 23-EMA two days running → partial; 50-DMA / 65-EMA → out; gap down below the 65-EMA; huge volume that stalls within days of a breakout → lighten | exit rules — THE LINE; Block 5's exit question |
| **Base quality** | proper base ≥ 5 weeks flat / ≥ 7 weeks other; too short, too fast up the right side, *"straight up off the bottom"*, stage-3, and wide-and-loose are all failure-prone; the third attempt often works because the crowd has given up | the consolidation family's base gates; the supply-ladder read |
| **Volume signatures** | dry-up in tight action; up-day volume above down-day volume; tight price on rising volume = accumulation, the opposite of churning | detectors (no money) |
| **RS** | RS line at new highs before price; outperforming while the market consolidates; breaking out before the market's follow-through day | theme north star: early RS before mainstream |
| **Fundamentals** | every page carries four quarters of EPS and revenue growth — mostly triple-digit | EP catalyst grading |

## What this does not answer
- **Failure rates.** Every chart is a winner; the same signals on names that did not lead are not shown.
- **Point-in-time reads.** The boxes were written with the full year visible. Some (*"ultimately get stopped out"*) could not have been known on the day.
- **Our bars.** None of the 13 can be scored against `mi_daily_closes` (history starts 2025-08-18) without pulling 2020 history first.
