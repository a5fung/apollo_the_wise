## KNOWN FAILURE MODES
- in-session frames can only be bounded by the day's low-high range (our bars are daily): a misread whose look-alike moved inside a similar range is not caught, and a replacement there is weaker evidence than one against an exact close-to-close session
- pre-market frames with TC2000's pre-market data on show the pre-market move, which our bars do not carry: they read as the session's range (approximately right near the open) or as no context
- a symbol with no bars (GBTC / ETHE before 2024, SPACs, renamed / delisted names our files lack) cannot be checked: it stays as read, flagged no_bars
- 1280x720 % cells misread digits (ACAD's -1.63% read -1.03%, which matched ACA within 0.15 pp): a replacement needs every % variant to agree AND a match within 0.02 pp AND a small enough search; a 1920x1080 read all variants agree on is never replaced - a mismatch there is kept (mismatch_kept: a misread % cell, or a name whose bars belong to another company under today's symbol)
- the frame's context is voted by its own rows: a frame with one or two readable rows, or a pre-market frame with the pre-market data on, may get no context (no_context: nothing checked)
- replacements are made only from tier 1 against an exact close-to-close session (on this run: 3/9 such attempts matched vs 1/33 on the null -> ~91% right); an in-session tier-1 match is only a suggestion (21/25 vs 15/47 on the null -> ~60% right) and a tier-2/3 look-alike match too (X tier 2: 23/163 vs 40/661) - suggestions carry pct_suggest / pct_suggest_why on the row
