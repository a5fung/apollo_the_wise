#622 follow-on -- "does our catalyst/EP scorer separate small-cap winners from
small-cap garbage?" (operator's own idea: score the 90 days we already have,
point-in-time, EOD, instead of waiting 60 forward days)

READ-ONLY measurement. No threshold, filter, or scoring code changed.

============================== HEADLINE ==============================

NO. On this sample, our own catalyst grader + rubric does not separate the
small-cap winners from the small-cap losers -- it runs essentially at a coin
flip, and on two of three readings it leans slightly the WRONG way (rating
losers a little higher than winners).

  ep_score (CURRENT live rubric, separation ON since 2026-08-22):
    winners (n=24): mean 46.9, median 37.5
    losers  (n=24): mean 51.4, median 37.5   <- losers score HIGHER on average
    AUC = 0.467  (0.5 = coin flip; below 0.5 = runs backwards)

  ep_score_legacy (the rubric actually live for 46 of these 48 scan dates,
  i.e. before the 2026-08-22 change):
    winners: mean 43.0, median 35.0
    losers:  mean 46.6, median 32.5          <- losers score higher again
    AUC = 0.476

  Live HIGH bar (65, current rubric) -- how many would the scorer alone have
  flagged, if the market-cap floor were the only thing removed:
    winners crossing:  2 of 24 (FBRX 2026-07-09, SENS 2026-08-07)
    losers  crossing:  4 of 24 (AGPU 2026-07-23, RGNX 2026-06-22,
                                 DUOT 2026-08-17, PESI 2026-08-12)
  MORE losers than winners would clear the bar. A pure "drop the cap floor,
  keep the score bar" lane would admit more garbage than winners.

  Legacy per-regime bar (65/70/75/80, the rubric actually live for 46 of 48
  scan dates) on the SAME 48 names: 6 winners vs 5 losers clear it -- still a
  coin flip, not a real catch (detail in the CHPT section below).

  Catalyst LABEL alone barely differs either: winners 8 strong + 2 game_changer
  of 24 (42%); losers 7 strong + 4 game_changer of 24 (46%). The grader is
  finding real, dated, material-sounding catalysts on plenty of the losers
  too -- this isn't a grading-quality problem, it's a "a real catalyst still
  doesn't predict the trade" problem.

============================== THE EXAMPLE THAT STARTED THIS: CHPT ==============================

CHPT (2026-09-03, $134M) is NOT one of the 24 winners in the sample above --
under the SAME sampling rule used for this probe, its 09:31-settled R was only
+0.33 (below the >+0.5R winner cutoff; by 09:36 it was already marked +2.0R
unrealized, still open). It is reported here separately as a named case study
of the specific stock that prompted this whole question, same pipeline, same
point-in-time rules -- not as a member of the winner group above:

  catalyst_quality = "strong" (correctly grounded: Q2 revenue +18% YoY beat,
    adjusted EBITDA loss narrowed $22.1M -> $4.8M, record 38% gross margin --
    a real, dated, material catalyst for a $134M name)
  ep_score (CURRENT rubric)  = 52.5  -- BELOW the 65 HIGH bar. The scorer
    would NOT have flagged his own example.
  ep_score_legacy (pre-8/22) = 80.0  -- comfortably clears every regime's old
    bar (65-80).

  WHY THE GAP, AND WHY THAT IS NOT "THE OLD SCORER WOULD HAVE CAUGHT IT":
  CHPT's gap was 49.8% -- huge. The CURRENT rubric flattened gap size to a
  flat 10 points (any qualifying gap, #533 2026-08-22, because gap SIZE ran
  BACKWARDS -- AUC 0.34 -- on the labelled real-EP set that change was
  measured against; this probe's own prior #622 finding agrees: gap size does
  not separate, 15.5% vs 14.9% medians). The OLD rubric paid a 20%+ gap 25
  points and would trip CHPT's conviction floor to 80, comfortably clearing
  its regime's bar. But checked against the SAME 48-name sample, the legacy
  rubric's bar-crossing is 6 winners vs 5 losers -- also a coin flip (AUC
  0.476 overall, see above). The legacy rubric does not single out CHPT; it
  rewards big gaps INDISCRIMINATELY, and on this sample that pays winners
  (BLZE, FBRX, DCTH, OPRT, SENS, QMCO) and losers (AII, DUOT 08-17, FF, PESI
  08-12, SILC) at essentially the same rate. CHPT clears the old bar not
  because the old rubric found something real about CHPT specifically, but
  because it paid every big gap, real catalyst or not -- the same mechanism
  that flags PESI 08-12 (-1.0R) and FF 08-11 (-1.0R). Reverting #533 would
  not fix separation; it would just admit more big-gap names of both kinds.

============================== WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT SUPPORT ==============================

- n=48 (24/24) clears the N>=10 CHANGE_PROCESS floor for evidence volume, but
  this is a NULL result (no separation, borderline-backwards), not a
  borderline one -- more data would sharpen a weak signal, it would not turn
  a coin-flip into a strong one on its own.
- It supports: "catalyst-quality score, as currently computed, is not a
  viable STANDALONE mechanism for widening the cap floor." Building a
  cap-relaxation lane on today's ep_score alone is not supported by this
  evidence.
- It does NOT support "no mechanism can ever work" -- only that score alone,
  on THIS 90-day window, does not. Gap size and ADV/liquidity were already
  shown (prior #622 work) not to separate either; market cap is still the
  only recorded feature that does.
- The #533 gap-flattening change is why today's score (52.5) falls short of
  the bar on CHPT specifically -- but the evidence here says reverting it
  would NOT fix separation: the pre-8/22 rubric is also a coin flip on this
  same 48-name sample (6 winners vs 5 losers clear its bar). It would catch
  CHPT by paying every big gap indiscriminately, real catalyst or not, which
  is the same mechanism that would re-admit PESI 08-12 and FF 08-11.
- The holistic judge (`ep_grade_judge`), which can further override the score
  tier including all the way to suppression, was NOT run here (scope
  decision -- see driver docstring) -- it could only narrow the "would have
  fired" set further, never widen it, so it would not change this NO answer.

============================== METHOD, BRIEFLY ==============================

Real production entry points, not reimplemented: `ep_detector._classify_catalyst_claude`
(the actual `ep_catalyst_grade` LLM call) feeding `ep_detector._score_ep` with
`ep_rubric.SCORE_WEIGHTS` (current) and `SCORE_WEIGHTS_LEGACY` (pre-8/22, free
to also compute since `_score_ep` is a pure function).

No lookahead: grounded text via `scripts._grounded_reconstruct.reconstruct_grounded_text`
(SEC 8-K/6-K filed <= scan date, Alpaca/Benzinga wires created_at <= 09:31 ET
that morning). Web/Perplexity was not run at all (can't be pinned to a past
date) -- there is no "contaminated" leg to flag; it's simply absent. Market
cap fed to the grader/scorer is the POINT-IN-TIME `mcap_m` from the #622
replay's feature capture, not a live lookup. Regime multiplier and theme
membership are read from `mi_market_regime`/`mi_themes` STRICTLY BEFORE the
scan date (both tables get that day's own row written that evening, in the
nightly pull -- at 09:31 ET only the prior day's row exists; this exactly
mirrors how the live scan's own `get_latest_regime()` behaves at premarket-
scan time). 2 of 48 rows (HELP 2026-07-16 winner, DUOT 2026-08-17 loser) carry
`sec_same_day_risk=True` -- the nearest SEC filing was dated the scan day
itself and either has no acceptance timestamp or an acceptance time after
09:31 ET, so those two grades carry a small residual same-day-timing caveat
(SEC's public feed is date- not minute-precise for older filings). Dropping
both (23 winners / 23 losers) moves the live-rubric AUC from 0.467 to 0.508
(closer to neutral, not further backwards) and bar-crossing to 2 winners / 3
losers -- the "no separation, if anything losers edge it" conclusion holds
either way.

Sample: all 24 winners (realized_r_0931 > +0.5) + a random sample of 24
losers (realized_r_0931 <= -0.9) matched to that count, `random.seed(622)`,
from the two degenerate-stop rows excluded per the task (RELL 2026-06-08,
AVBC 2026-07-24). Exact keys: `scripts/probes/_622score_sample.json`.

Simplifications (documented in the driver's docstring, not hidden):
vol_percentile defaulted to 50 (no bonus either way, +/-5pt max, not
directionally biased); the earnings Q-rev downgrade gate
(catalyst_metrics_extractor) was not run (can only lower a score, so this is
a slight upper-bound on a handful of earnings-catalyst rows, not a source of
the null result).
