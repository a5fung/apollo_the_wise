## Fixes (most severe first)

1. **Registration text, before freeze — §5 corporate-actions note, the `grp_pct` clause.** Replace "the group aggregate excludes
   DISCONTINUOUS tickers" with a sentence saying the stored `grp_pct` is used as built: SIC-2 median r63 over at least 10 members,
   percentile across groups (`CB:1698-1717`, `CB:794-804`). Only the event's own-ticker window rule (D-63, D] is applied. Add why:
   recomputing needs `cb702_sic.psv` and the runner's universe pass, and neither is on the §5 whitelist, so the doc's two
   sentences conflict. A median over ≥ 10 members limits what one jumping member can move. (Code: none. The alternative,
   putting the SIC file and a universe recompute on the whitelist, is a bigger build.)
2. **Registration text, before freeze — §5 guard 4.** Replace "every feature recomputed" with what runs. The 4 features the module
   computes, plus the DISC windows, are re-checked on a seeded 500-row sample with bars, watch rows and flag dates after D replaced

   the per-date cross-section. Optional instead: extend guard 4 to call `explain_watch` on bars cut at D. This needs a stand-in
   `o` column, because the export's `o` is forbidden.
3. **Registration text, before freeze — §5 flag source and the readings.** Name the era-B flags file the module actually reads,
