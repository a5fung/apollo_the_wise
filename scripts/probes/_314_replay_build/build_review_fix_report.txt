##### REVIEW 0 Do not freeze yet. The build mostly follows the round-4 plan: 40 tests pass, the dry run reads 93 of 93 claims OK, and the detector gates, the walk rules, the bootstrap and the consequence grid all match the plan. One high defect changes numbers widely. In era A, the check for a stock split between a filing's cover-page date and its filed date uses a tolerance of 1e-9. Alpaca's adjusted closes are rounded to the cent, so the raw-to-adjusted ratio drifts by about 1e-4 from one bar to the next on any name with a later forward split. Most such nights therefore read as having a split, which makes their market cap unknown. That moves them to the detector's mid tier instead of their real one, makes the P1 $250M floor row reject them, and makes their read-5 dilution unknown. It touches 1,797 of the 12,532 era-A symbols, which carry 13.8% of P1-band and 16% of P2-band nights. Four medium findings: (1) leftover alias rows fall back to today's holder's active record, which the plan forbids; (2) the name cross-check reads SEC names ending in a state tag such as "/DE/" as a different company; (3) the builder's own INBX / DOC refusal (R58) can stop a correct pipeline, because Polygon's delisting date usually lags the last trade by one to two sessions; (4) era-A news is looked up under today's symbol. The low findings are about wiring and clarity.
