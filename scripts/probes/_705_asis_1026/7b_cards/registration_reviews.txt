VERDICT: Not ready to freeze. As written, the most likely outcome is "no method qualifies", and the rules themselves cause it, not his trades. So 7b's answer A ("one style") would be the same reading whether we truly cannot see a group or the method is broken. The causes:
(1) Whitening caps how much a planted group can count in the k-means objective, below what the best pure-noise split gets in 18 dimensions. That makes M0, M2, M3 and M4 blind at the target cell by construction. Toy: M2 bootstrap ARI 0.03 whitened vs 0.86 unwhitened; full-fit ARI 0.04 vs 0.57.
(2) C2 is guaranteed to MISMATCH, and stop the run, at Δ 3.0, because the rank-normal map caps the post-map difference at 1.656.
(3) M3's null is never whitened, so p ≈ 1 always.
(4) The block plants shift one member of each collinear sweep, a shape no chart style has. Detectability then comes from the broken collinearity.
(5) The ARI ≥ 0.5 hit rule cannot be reached even by the Bayes oracle below a separation of about 2.0. So Δ 1.0 always fails, and Δ 1.5 fails whenever a block's columns are correlated.
(6) The B3 plant on hold also moves the masks, so B3 is partly a gap-pattern plant.
(7) Pass/fail lines at 100 and 120 sets are coin flips near the line.
Medium and low items: the C3, C4 and C5 checks are tautological or non-discriminating; R rests on 3-19 rows for four exit look-backs; a hard-coded sentence wrongly says every measure is bell-shaped after the map; and several method settings are underspecified.

[high] §5 (M0, M2, M3, M4 all on whitened components) and §8
  F: A whitening artefact and an honest 'cannot see' give the same reading: Fallback 2, then A. Whitening sets every kept component's variance to 1, so a planted direction can carry at most one component's worth of the k-means objective. For a 30% group its share is p(1-p)D²/(1+p(1-p)D²) = 0.59 at the target (D 2.6 if the three columns are unrelated). The best 2-means split of pure noise at n 658, m 18 already takes about 0.72 (1 - 0.9603, times 18). So no whitened k-means method can prefer the planted split below D ≈ 3.5, which is Δ 2.0 on three unrelated measures. Toy (30% planted at Δ 1.5 on 3 of 18 independent dimensions): full-fit ARI against truth is 0.04 whitened vs 0.57 unwhitened. M2's mean bootstrap ARI is 0.03 whitened vs 0.86 unwhitened. At Δ 2.0: 0.06 vs 0.83, and 0.03 vs 0.95. §8's expected qualifier ('M2 or M3') cannot pass as specified. The closed list tests 'unwhitened' only in M1, where it is tied to the half-sample rule, so the two diagnosed weaknesses are never separated.
  FIX: Separate the two diagnoses. Add M2u and M3u: M2 and M3 on M1's components (parallel analysis, unwhitened). M3u then takes standard SigClust form: null draws from N(0, diag of the kept eigenvalues), passed through the same reduction. The list becomes M1, M2w, M2u, M3w, M3u, M4, selected in that order. N1 decides whether the unwhitened cuts invent groups along his first component. Correct §8's expectation and the BUILD NOTE's 'whitening is not the cause': that was tested on a 50/50 world only.

[high] §5 M3 (null and level) and §11's M3 test
